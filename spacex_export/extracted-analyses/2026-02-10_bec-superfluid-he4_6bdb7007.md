## Assistant

A phonon is a quasiparticle—a quantum of collective vibrational energy in a crystal lattice—arising when atoms oscillate around equilibrium positions due to thermal motion or interactions, treated as harmonic normal modes in solid-state quantum mechanics. To quantize, expand lattice displacements in Fourier modes, map to a Hamiltonian of decoupled harmonic oscillators ∑ ω_k (a†_k a_k + 1/2), where a†_k creates a phonon of wavevector k and frequency ω_k, behaving as spin-zero bosons in a quantum field theory of the displacement field, much like photons for electromagnetic waves. Distinguish acoustic phonons (sound-like, linear dispersion) from optical ones (opposing sublattice motion in multi-atom cells); virtual phonons in scattering are off-shell excitations mediating forces without conserving energy-momentum strictly.
