### Core Experiment: Kim et al. (1999/2000)
The foundational experiment was reported in the paper **"A Delayed Choice Quantum Eraser"** by Yoon-Ho Kim, R. Yu, S.P. Kulik, Y.H. Shih, and Marlan O. Scully (submitted 1999, published 2000 in *Physical Review Letters*).

- **Setup**: It uses spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC) in a BBO crystal to generate entangled signal-idler photon pairs. The signal photon goes through a double-slit-like setup (or equivalent) and is detected at D0 (which can show interference). The idler photon travels a longer path, and a delayed choice is made (via beam splitters and detectors D1–D4) to either preserve or erase "which-path" information about the signal photon.
- **Key Result**: The choice to erase or not erase which-path info (made *after* the signal photon is detected) determines whether the *coincidence* counts (correlated detections) show an interference pattern or not. The overall pattern at D0 is always a broad lump (no interference), but subsets selected by idler measurements reveal fringes or anti-fringes.
- **Interpretation**: This demonstrates quantum complementarity and entanglement. It does *not* involve retrocausality (the future changing the past) or signaling backward in time. The "erasure" works via post-selection on correlated data. The total (unselected) data shows no interference.

The idea builds on earlier proposals by Scully and Drühl (1982) and Wheeler's delayed-choice concepts.

### Updates and Later Developments (2000s–2026)
- **Undergraduate/Accessible Versions** (2016+): Simplified optical setups using polarization-entangled photons for teaching, adding delay stages to ensure the erasure choice happens after signal detection.
- **Causally Disconnected / Loophole-Free** (2013): Ma et al. enforced Einstein locality with space-like separation (e.g., 144 km in some variants) to rule out any hidden signaling.
- **Coherent Photons** (2023): Kim and Ham demonstrated DCQE using coherent photon pairs with a polarizer for delayed choice.
- **Other Extensions**: Combinations with Hong-Ou-Mandel interference, solid-state versions, kaon systems, and quantum-controlled beam splitters. Analyses in forward-time only (no retrocausality needed).
- **Clarifications & Debunking Popular Misconceptions** (2020s): Many explanations (including videos and articles up to 2025–2026) emphasize that there's no rewriting of the past. The patterns arise from entanglement correlations revealed by post-selection. Classical analogs using correlated dice/coins can mimic the statistics without quantum effects.
