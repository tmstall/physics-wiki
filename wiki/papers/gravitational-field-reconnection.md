# Reconnection of Gravitational Field Sheets

**One-line summary:** Comisso and Asenjo define gravitational reconnection as the failure mode of their frozen-in tetrad sheets, with a plasma-style rate and a slippage test; no spacetime is computed, and the sheets depend on the observer frame. *Phys. Rev. D* (2026).

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Luca Comisso and Felipe A. Asenjo. DOI [10.1103/lmqy-pf9k](https://doi.org/10.1103/lmqy-pf9k). arXiv:2609.25241v1. The journal version was not read. Sequel to their 2026 PRL with Winkler on ideal (frozen-in) gravitational flux and helicity, page [[frozen-in-gravitational-fields]].
- Each leg of a local tetrad is treated as a potential. Field strengths measure how the frame fails to close. Einstein’s equations take a Maxwell-like form sourced by matter and by the Sparling current. A non-ideal term is the analog of resistivity.
- Slippage versus reconnection: a non-ideal term that can be absorbed into a new transport flow does not change connectivity. True reconnection needs a term that cannot be absorbed (a gravitational “electric” piece along the “magnetic” field, or a breakdown of the transport flow). Rates are a separator line integral, or the maximum parallel-field integral through the non-ideal region. Helicity can be created or destroyed. A 3+1 split looks like resistive MHD, including a gravitational Reynolds-like number.
- No black hole, wave, or merger is evaluated. The press line that energy moves from gravity into matter is stronger than the paper’s qualitative, frame-dependent exchange density.

## Physical intuition

In a perfect plasma, magnetic lines are painted on the gas. Resistivity lets them break and rejoin, which is a solar flare. The earlier paper painted gravitational sheets onto a flow when an ideal condition holds. This paper writes the flare: when that condition fails, ask whether the sheets merely slip onto a different flow or actually change which regions they connect. The test is the useful object. The rewrite of Einstein’s equations is exact, so it adds no new law by itself.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Field sheets, flux, rate, and helicity depend on the tetrad. Gravitational energy is not locally frame-invariant. Whether freely falling frames make the topology physical is open.
- In the paper’s adapted frames, ideal evolution needs a free-falling time symmetry. Schwarzschild and dynamical spacetimes lack it, so non-ideality is generic and the slippage test does all the work. That reading is an analyst derivation; the PRL was not re-read here.
- The non-ideal term is a placeholder (singularities, coarse-graining, quantum backreaction, modified gravity), not a model.
- Four tetrad legs each have their own sheets. How those connectivities talk to each other is not discussed.
