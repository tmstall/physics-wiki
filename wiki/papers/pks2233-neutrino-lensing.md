# PKS 2233−148: IceCube Neutrino Candidate with Possible Lensing Signatures

**One-line summary:** Multi-wavelength + VLBA study of the leading southern IceCube blazar candidate documents jet jumps, a nested gamma-ray flare, and a symmetric radio hump — Bayesian tests reject lensing for the flash; remaining self-/micro-lensing ideas stay illustrative and unconstrained (MNRAS Advance Access).

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Britzen et al., MNRAS Advance Access, DOI 10.1093/mnras/stag1355 (peer-reviewed Open Access).
- IceCube context: strongest southern AGN neutrino hotspot among 110 γ-selected candidates (~5.3 excess events, pre-trial $p=0.06$) but **post-trial $p=0.55$** — local standout, not airtight.
- New Fe Kα redshift $z=0.63^{+0.04}_{-0.03}$ (3.1σ stacked Swift-XRT) — best current estimate, mild tension with older $z>0.65$ lower limit.
- VLBA: ~0.3 mas gradual jet shift; separate ~1.8 mas perpendicular jump (2010–2012) with Paczyński-like brightening → illustrative asteroid-mass ($\sim2.8\times10^{-8}\,M_\odot$) microlensing mass if velocity assumptions hold.
- Gamma-ray “flare-in-flare”: Bayesian evidence **decisively rejects** two-image lensing ($\Delta\ln Z\sim-14$ to $-26$) vs two-component jet flare.
- OVRO ~8 yr symmetric double-hump: self-lensing SMBH-binary N-body is **illustrative**, not a formal fit; symmetry unlikely under pure stochastic noise.
- Full macrolensing ruled out (no split VLBI images); external microlensing by known absorbers remains open/untested.

## Physical intuition

Finding which blazar shoots IceCube neutrinos is still messy statistics. This paper’s discipline is the point: the feature that *looks* most like lensing fails a proper model comparison, while stranger leftover anomalies get carefully hedged exotic stories (hidden binary, maybe compact dark microlenses) without overselling confirmation.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Single-source case study; “neutrino blazars show lensing” framing leans on same group’s recent papers (PKS 1717+177, TXS 0506+056).
- Microlensing/PBH mass needs unconstrained transverse velocity; optical depth unconstrained — **not** positive PBH-DM evidence.
- Binary self-lensing parameters not independently pinned.
- Redshift at 3.1σ, not 5σ.
