# Evaporating Charged Black Holes Avoid Singularities

**One-line summary:** Charge (or spin) plus Hawking radiation’s null-energy violation can erase both curvature singularities and Cauchy horizons in several semiclassical evaporation endpoints — regular black-hole interiors without new gravity or exotic matter.

## Key claims and results

- Author framed as Di Filippo (analysis); charged spherical collapse with dynamical mass/charge under Hawking evaporation.
- Classically, Reissner–Nordström-type collapse can bounce inside the inner horizon yet still form a Cauchy horizon.
- Hawking flux violates the null energy condition, allowing the outer horizon to become timelike so trapped matter can escape.
- Five Penrose-diagram endpoints; **three are fully regular** (no singularity, no Cauchy horizon): complete evaporation in finite time, asymptotic complete evaporation, horizonless remnant.
- Two endpoints (extremal remnant / asymptotic extremal) remain pathological.
- Rotating (Kerr) case sketched: centrifugal repulsion may replace charge.

## Physical intuition

Penrose-type theorems say trapped surfaces + energy conditions force incompleteness. Hawking radiation already breaks the null energy condition with an ingoing negative-energy flux. Charge supplies a repulsive bounce so matter need not crunch at \(r=0\). Team those two effects and the trapped region can disappear cleanly — matter flies out into a regular future.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Relies on bounce-friendly matter models (dust / thin shells); realistic equations of state may differ.
- Mass inflation and full back-reaction not calculated; Planck-scale physics may pick which endpoint occurs.
- Astrophysical black holes are nearly neutral — spin is the realistic analog, still incomplete.
- Logical possibility within semiclassical GR, not a proof that nature chooses regular endpoints.
