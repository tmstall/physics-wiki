# Two Jets, One Core: Mrk 501 as a Close SMBH Binary

**One-line summary:** Twelve years of 43 GHz VLBA monitoring of blazar Mrk 501 reveal a second jet looping around the primary—direct imaging evidence for two central engines in one core, with a binary deep inside the final-parsec regime and possible PTA-relevant merger timescales.

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Britzen et al., *MNRAS* (March 2026) (per analysis).
- 83 epochs of VLBA at 43 GHz (2011–2023); Gaussian component fitting with careful uncertainty profiling.
- **Jet 2** loops counterclockwise around Jet 1 (~121 day period in analysis); whole system precesses on ~7 yr scale.
- June 2022 Einstein-ring-like event supports binary lensing geometry (foreground BH lenses background jet/engine).
- Inferred separation tens to ~10² Schwarzschild radii class (analysis: ~27–128 \(R_S\)); masses ~10⁸–10⁹ \(M_\odot\) each.
- Claims pair may merge on ~century scales depending on masses—prime PTA continuous-wave / burst target narrative.
- Demonstrates **jet kinematics** as a binary probe when EHT cannot spatially resolve the holes themselves.

## Physical intuition

One black hole, one jet. Two holes, two jets—and if they orbit, one jet’s path should weave around the other like a garden sprinkler on a merry-go-round. You may never see the engines as two dots, but a decade of radio movies can still catch the dance.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Blazar beaming geometry and projection complicate mass/period inference.
- Alternative single-engine precession / jet-internal structure models need continuous exclusion.
- Merger-time estimates highly mass- and eccentricity-dependent.
- “First direct imaging of double jet = binary” claim is strong; community confirmation campaigns matter.
