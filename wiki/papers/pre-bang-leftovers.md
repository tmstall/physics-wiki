# Pre-Bang Leftovers: Bounce Dark Matter

**One-line summary:** In a bouncing cosmology, objects larger than ~90 m can exit the shrinking particle horizon before the bounce and reappear as relic black holes, gravitational waves, and possible dark-matter candidates — a “bounce dark matter” scenario for pre-Bang debris.

## Key claims and results

- Author framing: Gaztañaga (analysis; arXiv:2602.17702 cited).
- Bounce from phenomenological relativistic degeneracy pressure (not full quantum gravity).
- Survival filter: perturbations/compact objects above ~90 m become super-horizon near the bounce.
- Two BDM channels: (1) direct survival of pre-bounce compact objects/GWs; (2) halo collapse to BHs upon post-bounce horizon re-entry.
- Broad relic BH mass spectrum proposed; possible link to early JWST “little red dots” / SMBH seeds.
- Claims consistency with inflationary spectrum + late acceleration (verify against paper).

## Physical intuition

Standard Big Bang singularity is a model failure notice, not a photo of the beginning. A bounce is a rebound from a dense but finite state. Near the bounce the causal “city limit” shrinks; buildings larger than the new limit sit outside the firewall and cannot be scrambled. They reappear after expansion as leftovers — black holes and waves that never needed rare inflationary PBH flips of a coin.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Bounce mechanism phenomenological, not derived from UV quantum gravity.
- Highly speculative relative to standard ΛCDM + particle DM.
- Mass spectrum and CMB parity claims need independent checks.
- Competition with ordinary PBH and particle DM scenarios.
