## Physical intuition

Gravitational-wave detectors have a coverage gap: ground-based detectors like LIGO catch frequencies above about 10 Hz, the future space-based LISA will catch frequencies below about 0.1 Hz, and nothing covers the middle. Atom interferometers — built from two separated clouds of ultracold atoms sharing one laser — are the leading proposal to fill that gap, and the same setup is also sensitive to a class of dark matter candidate.
