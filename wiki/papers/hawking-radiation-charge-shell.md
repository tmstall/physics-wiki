# Hawking Radiation from a Charge Shell (Double Copy)

**One-line summary:** The electromagnetic single copy of a collapsing Vaidya shell, after all-order eikonal ladder resummation in flat space, yields thermal Bogoliubov coefficients whose temperature double-copies to the Vaidya Hawking temperature.

## Key claims and results

- Classical double copy: Kerr-Schild map replaces gravitational mass parameter with charge → time-dependent Coulomb shell (single copy of Vaidya).
- Scatter a massless charged scalar off that background; ladders exponentiate in the geometric-optics limit because \(A^2=0\) (no seagulls) and the Kerr-Schild structure factors.
- Resummed phase \(\propto (-v/\mu)^{2i\alpha}\) Fourier-transforms to Gamma-function amplitudes; \(|\beta/\alpha|^2\) is Planckian with temperature set by \(\alpha = QQ_B/(4\pi)\).
- Same coefficients from null-ray tracing / eikonal action in the same EM background.
- Thermality is the double-copy image of the original black hole’s surface gravity — computed without leaving flat space.

## Physical intuition

Double copy says gravity is often the “square” of gauge theory. Here the authors run the dictionary backward: build an EM stand-in for a collapsing shell, do ordinary flat-space QFT, and recover the thermal particle spectrum that gravity would call Hawking radiation. One fiber-optic cable (the shared null direction) carries both the single-copy field and, after squaring, the metric perturbation.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Kerr-Schild / null collapse only; Kerr and charged rotating cases open.
- Central charge held by external force — no dynamical EM back-reaction.
- Eikonal limit only; no full entropy counting.
