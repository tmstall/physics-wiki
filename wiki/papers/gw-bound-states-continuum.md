# Bound States in the Continuum of Gravitational Waves

> **Exploratory / thin primary access.** Analysis rests on **abstract + metadata only** (full text paywalled, no arXiv found). Treat as a formalism pointer, not established GW phenomenology.

**One-line summary:** Berté (*Physica Scripta* 2026) constructs a linearized-GR analog of an optical bound state in the continuum (BIC): a localized, periodic spacetime strain that coincides in $(k,\omega)$ with radiating GW modes but is symmetry-decoupled from them, anchored by an idealized surface stress-energy sheet.

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Rodrigo Berté, *Physica Scripta* **101**, 325001 (2026), DOI 10.1088/1402-4896/ae92db.
- Transplants photonic/acoustic BIC idea into **linearized** vacuum GR.
- Localized periodic metric perturbation satisfies vacuum linearized Einstein equations except on one plane (distributional surface stress-energy).
- At the Γ point, overlaps propagating GW polarization continuum but fails to couple due to opposite symmetry under 90° in-plane rotation (symmetry-protected BIC mechanism).
- Framed as first GW-BIC existence proof; **no** LIGO/source prediction in the abstract-level analysis.

## Physical intuition

Optics can trap light where it “should” leak by symmetry mismatch. This paper asks whether weak-field gravity allows the same bookkeeping for spacetime ripples — a trapped strain that sits in the radiation continuum but cannot radiate. Anchoring still needs an idealized infinite sheet of stress-energy; nature may or may not build that.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Full derivation not verified in this wiki ingest (abstract-only source analysis).
- Idealized surface stress-energy; physical realizability open.
- Linearized GR only — far from BH merger regime.
- No empirical handle in available material — formalism/existence claim.
