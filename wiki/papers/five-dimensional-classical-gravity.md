# Five-Dimensional Classical Framework for Gravity and Quantum Phenomena

> **Exploratory / non-consensus.** This page catalogs a speculative foundations proposal. It is **not** community-standard GR or quantum gravity. Claims that classical 5D worldlines “are” entanglement or interference remain unproven equivalence statements.

**One-line summary:** Strubbe (*Scientific Reports* 2025) proposes a purely classical 5D manifold with evolution parameter $\tau$ in which worldline density sources an Einstein-like metric; equilibrium recovers GR-like gravity while zigzag worldlines and multi-worldline bundles are claimed to emulate entanglement and interference without quantizing the metric.

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Filip Strubbe, *Scientific Reports* (2025), DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-32860-8.
- Strategy inverted from quantum gravity: keep dynamics classical; add a fifth coordinate $\tau$ that parametrizes relaxation of 4D slices and particle worldlines.
- Gravity sourced by worldline stress-energy; as $\tau$ advances the system relaxes toward equilibria whose effective 4D metric matches GR features (weak-field Poisson, light bending, time dilation in simulations).
- Quantum-like targets (claimed, not standard QFT): EPR-style correlations via Costa de Beauregard–type zigzags in $\tau$; double-slit interference via multi-worldline path bundles that phase during relaxation.
- Weak-gravity numerical integrations show worldline bundling and metric stabilization toward Newtonian orbits.
- Positions itself against string/LQG “quantize gravity” programs; offers distinguishable signatures in gravity-mediated entanglement tests (theory-side claim).

## Physical intuition

Instead of quantizing the metric, let spacetime and particle threads co-relax in an extra evolution coordinate until they freeze into something that looks like our 4D GR world. Entanglement becomes shared history plus zigzags in that hidden parameter — nonlocal in the 4D movie, local in the 5D edit suite. Interference is many classical threads fanning out, then settling on a detection spot. Whether that truly *is* quantum mechanics is the open bet; the engineering pitch is “classical self-assembly that mimics both.”

## Limitations and assumptions

- **Status:** exploratory single-author-class foundations paper; not a working alternative to GR + QFT used in precision tests.
- Flat 5D start + ad-hoc worldline initials; strong-field / black-hole relaxation convergence unproven.
- No fermions, spin, or gauge symmetries yet — Dirac/QED sector missing.
- Quantum-like claims are simulation/analogy level, not proven equivalence to QM Hilbert-space structure or Bell-test phenomenology.
- Numerical work limited to weak gravity; instabilities in stronger regimes not fully reported.
- Do not cite this page as established physics; use it only as a pointer to an idea under discussion.
