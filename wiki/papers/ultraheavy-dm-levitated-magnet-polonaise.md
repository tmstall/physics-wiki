# Ultraheavy Dark Matter Search with a Levitated Milligram Magnet (Polonaise)

**One-line summary:** First dedicated search for ultraheavy DM (cell-scale masses) via levitated mg-scale magnet; ~7 orders better high-mass sensitivity than ng optical traps via coherent neutron response; null result with conservative treatment of 8 blips → exclusion limits. arXiv:2608.20464.

## Key claims and results

- POLONAISE retargets a milligram magnetically levitated ferromagnet (0.356 mg) from ultralight-wave searches to single-kick ultraheavy DM (roughly $10^6$–$10^{15}$ GeV/$c^2$).
- Long-range coherent neutron response (~$10^{20}$ neutrons) plus contact-free SQUID readout yields leading high-mass reach; force sensitivity ~0.07 fN/√Hz, quality factor >2.25 million.
- ~9.15 days of quiet nighttime data; eight candidate impulses (1.5–13 TeV/$c$) treated as possible signal, not subtracted.
- Optimum-interval method → conservative 95% CL exclusion; for light mediators ($m_\phi \ll 1$ meV/$c^2$), $\alpha_n$ down to $3.2\times10^{-9}$ across ~$4\times10^5$–$8\times10^{14}$ GeV/$c^2$.
- High-mass ceiling extends ~7 orders beyond prior nanogram optical-levitation UHDM searches.

## Physical intuition

WIMP tanks count many soft hits. Ultraheavy DM is so sparse that you wait for one hard kick. A milligram floating magnet acts as a coherent force antenna: when the mediator range exceeds the target size, all neutrons respond in phase, so a heavier target both gets hit more often and kicks harder than a nanogram bead.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Unreviewed arXiv preprint — adversarial posture; treat as provisional.
- Eight unexplained blips lack a validated background model; limits over-cover by design.
- Exclusion assumes light-boson, neutron-coupling Yukawa force — not gravity-only UHDM.
- Single apparatus, single collaboration; no independent cross-check yet.
- 8.5% calibration uncertainty not folded into the quoted limit.
