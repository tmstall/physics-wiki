# Color Superconductivity: The Inevitable Dense-QCD Phase

**One-line summary:** Cooper’s theorem plus attractive color-antitriplet gluon exchange makes quark pairing at high density essentially inevitable; neutron-star cores are the only cold laboratory for color-superconducting phases (2SC, CFL, crystalline) on the QCD phase diagram.

## Key claims and results

- **Source type:** List2 deep-dive tutorial (session synthesis on color SC / QCD phase diagram), sparked by η′-mesic nucleus work—not a single standalone journal article. Treat as curated physics notes filed into the wiki.
- Cooper theorem: any Fermi surface + any attractive channel → pairing instability at \(T\to 0\) (log-divergent susceptibility).
- Quarks: one-gluon exchange attractive in color \(\bar{3}\); no phonon “accident” required (cleaner than electronic BCS in that sense).
- Gap scale at NS-core densities: often quoted ~10–100 MeV → \(T_c\) up to ~\(10^{11}\) K; cores cool below this within seconds of formation.
- **CFL** (asymptotic three-flavor): color and flavor lock; chiral symmetry broken; possible quark–hadron continuity at \(T=0\).
- Phase diagram corners: QGP (hot), hadronic (cold dilute), color SC zoo (cold dense); critical endpoint at finite \(\mu_B\) still unknown (sign problem blocks lattice).
- Observational handles: tidal deformability, mass–radius (NICER), cooling, glitches—all probe the same dense-matter corner.

## Physical intuition

A sharp Fermi surface is a razor edge: any weak attraction piles infinite phase space for pairing and reorganizes the ground state. Electrons need lattice phonons to overcome Coulomb hate. Quarks get attraction free from QCD’s color algebra. Neutron stars are permanent, un-shut-off experiments at \(\sim 10^{14}\)× nuclear density—LIGO and X-ray telescopes just read the package datasheet.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Gap and phase boundaries at realistic densities are model-dependent; pQCD is asymptotic, chiral EFT bottoms out below the core.
- Sign problem: no first-principles lattice map of the cold dense quadrant.
- Whether real NS cores host CFL, 2SC, hyperons, or continuous hadronic matter remains open—2 \(M_\odot\) stars constrain soft EOS.
- Tutorial source: no single DOI; cross-check textbooks / reviews for numbers.
