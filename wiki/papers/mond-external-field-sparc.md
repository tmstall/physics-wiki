# MOND’s Cosmic Fingerprint (External Field Effect on SPARC)

**One-line summary:** Fitting SPARC rotation curves with a free external-field parameter, then comparing to the Newtonian field computed from ~54k galaxies + ~1.7k clusters within 150 Mpc, finds consistent EFE and >4σ-class tension with the Strong Equivalence Principle as usually assumed in GR+DM.

## Key claims and results

- 162 SPARC galaxies; MOND fit floats dimensionless external field \(g_\mathrm{ext}/a_0\).
- Independent catalog sum of baryons → Newtonian environment field.
- Void galaxies: flatter outer curves; supercluster neighbors: declining—MOND EFE pattern.
- Two independent \(g_\mathrm{ext}\) measurements agree (fit vs census).
- Interpreted as SEP violation signature of MOND; hard for isolated-halo DM without environment coupling.

## Physical intuition

In Newton/GR, a lab doesn’t care about the gravity of the Virgo cluster for local dynamics (SEP). MOND does: the ambient field can push the force law toward Newtonian. Outer rotation curves become a radio that broadcasts the neighborhood’s field strength.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Environment mass catalog incompleteness and distance errors.
- MOND interpolating function choices.
- Selection of SPARC subsample; DM models with environment coupling not exhaustively ruled out.
- Large-scale force law still prefers \(n\approx 2\) in [[newton-ksz-force-law]]—different scale/test.
