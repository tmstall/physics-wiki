# GLIMPSE-17775: Little Red Dot Super-Eddington Cocoon

**One-line summary:** Ultra-deep JWST spectroscopy of LRD GLIMPSE-17775 converges on a modest-mass black hole accreting super-Eddington, buried in a dense gas cocoon that inflated earlier mass estimates by roughly an order of magnitude.

## Key claims and results

- Target: “Little Red Dot” at high redshift with V-shaped SED (blue rest-UV, sharp red rest-optical upturn).
- ~80-hour-class JWST spectrum (analysis framing) forces multi-line evidence onto a **BH\* / cocoon** picture rather than overmassive stellar galaxy or pure dust.
- Dense partially ionized gas scatters like milk glass: X-rays absorbed, broad lines reshaped, continuum reprocessed — observers overestimate $M_{\rm BH}$ if they ignore the blanket.
- Super-Eddington accretion for a *smaller* hole fits better than a $10\times$ heavier hole at modest Eddington ratio.
- Challenges early-universe assembly tension: less stellar mass in the host than naive LRD-as-galaxy models required.

## Physical intuition

Little Red Dots looked like impossible baby galaxies or dust monsters. Neither survives multi-messenger checks. The winning picture is a black hole eating too fast, wrapped in a fog so thick it rewrites the spectrum. You thought you were weighing a heavyweight; you were weighing a lightweight in a weighted suit. The cocoon is the suit.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Single ultra-deep object; population fraction of cocoon LRDs still open.
- Continuum/decomposition systematics can trade mass against covering factor.
- Analysis-based ingest; confirm quantitative mass and Eddington ratio on the primary paper.
- Connection to local NLS1 growth mode is analogy, not identity.
