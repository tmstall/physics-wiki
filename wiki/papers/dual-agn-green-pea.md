# Dual AGN in a Green Pea Galaxy (J1622+3521)

**One-line summary:** Chandra + Keck confirm two independently accreting SMBHs (~$2\times10^7\,M_\odot$ each, 8.4 kpc apart) inside the compact, metal-poor Green Pea J1622+3521 — first confirmed dual AGN in that local early-universe analog class (arXiv-only at analysis time).

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Kouroumpatzakis et al., arXiv:2607.17729v1 (2026). **Adversarial posture:** no journal reference found at analysis — treat as unreviewed.
- Host: SDSS J162209.41+352107.5 at $z\approx0.267$; Green Pea (compact, metal-poor, intense SF) with merger-like morphology.
- Chandra (~42.7 ks): two hard X-ray sources 1.9″ apart → 8.4 kpc projected; joint BNsphere + BXA/MultiNest fit with cross-contamination terms.
- Intrinsic $\log(L_{2-10}/{\rm erg\,s^{-1}})\approx43.93$ and $43.55$; columns $\log N_{\rm H}\approx22.8$ and $23.6$ (obscured, not Compton-thick).
- Keck/DEIMOS: two nuclei with broad Hα (~1700–1900 km/s), coronal lines (He II, [Ne V], [Fe VII]), both AGN on BPT.
- Virial masses $\log(M_{\rm BH}/M_\odot)\simeq7.3$ each; Eddington ratios ~0.66 and ~0.29 (systematic scatter ~0.4–0.5 dex not in quoted errors).
- Velocity difference $7\pm74$ km/s — same redshift, **not** a measured orbit.

## Physical intuition

Green Peas are nearby staging grounds for “early-universe-like” compact, metal-poor starbursts. Dual AGN were previously confirmed almost only in massive gas-rich mergers. This is a proof-of-existence that two feeding holes can light up in a tiny Green Pea mid-merger — relevant to how JWST’s compact high-$z$ AGN might grow, not a census that such duals are common.

## Limitations and assumptions

- $N=1$ discovery; comparison benchmarks partly from same author program.
- Angular separation near Chandra PSF → blending modeled, not trivial.
- Spherical BNsphere obscurer “difficult to sustain”; photon index largely prior-dominated.
- Single-epoch virial masses carry large systematic scatter.
- arXiv-only at ingest — peer-review status open.
