# Gravity as a Compression Error (GfE Thermodynamics)

**One-line summary:** Bianconi’s Gravity-From-Entropy program replaces Einstein–Hilbert with a geometric quantum relative entropy between the true metric and a matter/curvature-induced metric; in approximate FRW cosmologies the local entropy density falls while total entropy rises — a horizon-free thermo story that still imports strong assumptions (*Phys. Rev. D* **114**, 024042 lineage per analysis).

## Key claims and results

- **Paper / program:** G. Bianconi — GfE framework (2025+) and this thermodynamics extension (PRD 114, 024042 per Claude analysis). Single-author-led, actively developing, **not** mainstream consensus.
- **Action:** $L=-\mathrm{Tr}_F\ln(\tilde G\,\tilde g^{-1})$ — KL-like penalty between higher-order true metric $\tilde g$ and induced $\tilde G=\tilde g+\alpha\tilde M-\beta\tilde R$. Perfect match → gravity “off.” Low-energy limit recovers Einstein–Hilbert with coupling ratio fixed to $\alpha_0/(3\beta_0)=16\pi$ (one free parameter left).
- **G-field:** Legendre multiplier dressing the metric; generates dynamical dark-energy-like term $\Lambda_G$ read as internal energy $E=2\beta\Lambda_G$.
- **FRW / mean-field insert:** Five geometric channels (scalar / vectors / bivectors) each get a k-temperature $\theta_k\approx u_k$ and a first law $\delta\epsilon_k=\theta_k\delta s_k-\pi_k\delta v$. With Friedmann insert $u_k=\omega_k H^2$: local entropy density $\sim H^2\sim t^{-2}$, energy density $\sim H^4\sim t^{-4}$; volume growth makes **total** $S$ diverge while total $E$ saturates.
- **de Sitter:** Restrict to causal diamond $V\sim H^{-4}$ → $S\sim H^{-2}$ (Gibbons–Hawking *scaling*); k-temperatures $\propto H^2$ (steeper than $T_{\rm dS}=H/2\pi$) and can be **negative**.

## Physical intuition

Horizon thermo (Bekenstein–Jacobson) puts entropy on an area. GfE puts it in a **volume density**: gravity is the extra bits you pay to encode the real metric with a codebook built from matter+curvature — a compression-error bill. Expansion dilutes that local bill while the growing volume makes the *integrated* entropy climb, so local order (galaxies) can coexist with a rising global second-law tally without needing a horizon cash register for that particular story.

## Limitations and assumptions

- **Fringe / single-lineage:** no independent derivation of the GQRE action; entropic gravity as a genre is contested.
- FRW results are **mean-field inserts** (G-field → identity), not exact GfE cosmologies — validity window $t\gg t_0$, $H\ll H_0$.
- Total-$S$ growth is kinematic (volume vs density), not a proven H-theorem.
- Link to Araki / type-III entanglement entropy is conjectural; analysis flags first-quantization development so far.
- k-temperatures ≠ Unruh/Hawking temperatures felt by quantum fields.
