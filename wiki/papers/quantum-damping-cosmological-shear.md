# Quantum Damping of Cosmological Shear (mLQC-I)

**One-line summary:** Modified loop quantum cosmology (mLQC-I) can erase Bianchi-I anisotropy in under a Planck time after a bounce—but the damping tracks classical cosmic no-hair driven by an emergent Planck-scale cosmological constant, and the post-bounce state may stay non-classical.

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Anzhong Wang et al., *Phys. Rev. Lett.* **136**, 251501 (23 Jun 2026). DOI: 10.1103/f8tr-bq61.
- Setting: anisotropic Bianchi I + mLQC-I effective dynamics (numerical + perturbative).
- Claim: shear damps extremely fast after the bounce without inflation or exotic matter.
- Critical reading (analysis + Motaharfar & Singh critique arXiv:2603.18175; author reply arXiv:2604.08912): damping rate \(\approx 3H\)—standard **Hubble friction / Wald no-hair**, powered by a large emergent \(\Lambda\).
- Cost: post-bounce curvature invariants can remain Planckian—**isotropization vs classicality** tension.
- Active dispute: whether this is a feature of quantum geometry or a steep price tag.

## Physical intuition

A bouncing universe is like a rubber band that can snap back lopsided. mLQC-I appears to comb the lopsidedness out instantly. Zoom in, and the comb is the old cosmic no-hair theorem: rapid expansion stretches anisotropy away—here the expansion is driven by a Planck-sized “dark energy” the quantum model grows for free. Smooth hair; possibly permanently quantum scalp.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Effective Hamiltonian already derived; not a first-principles LQG calculation of the bounce.
- No clean exit to a classical FLRW with small \(\Lambda\).
- Dust/matter content and full perturbation spectrum open.
- Interpretation contested in published critique/reply exchange.
