# Free-Space Optical Hall Effect via Pancharatnam Topological Charge

**One-line summary:** A single integer — Pancharatnam topological charge $\ell_P$ — dials concentric rings of opposite circular polarization in a fully paraxial free-space beam, controlling chirality without metasurfaces or tight focusing.

## Key claims and results

- Wits/UEA team (analysis framing): topology of the beam’s polarization path on the Poincaré sphere replaces material engineering.
- Free-space optical Hall effect: opposite-handed regions appear as concentric rings set by $\ell_P$.
- Works in low-NA / paraxial regime — no high-NA geometric spin-orbit crutches required.
- Master dial for spin–orbit structure of light before matter interaction.

## Physical intuition

Usually you buy chirality from fabrication: q-plates, metasurfaces, plasmonic edges. Here the beam carries its own winding number in polarization space. Sweep azimuth around the beam and the polarization state wraps the Poincaré sphere $\ell_P$ times; that integer forces left- and right-circular light into separate radial homes. Topology is the firmware; matter is optional.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Analysis-based ingest; verify measured Stokes maps and $\ell_P$ scaling on the primary paper.
- Material coupling / molecular enantioselectivity still a next step (see twisted-light MS).
- Broadband / polychromatic behavior may degrade ideal monochromatic topology.
