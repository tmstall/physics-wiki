# HOLISMOKES XIX & XX: Lensed SLSN Winny

**One-line summary:** First galaxy-scale strongly lensed superluminous supernova (SN 2025wny / Winny at \(z\approx 2\)) with five images behind a binary lens — the observational foundation for future time-delay \(H_0\) cosmography.

## Key claims and results

- **XIX** (Taubenberger et al., arXiv:2510.21694): discovery/classification via HOLISMOKES lens-catalog × transient alerts; \(z_\mathrm{SN}=2.008\pm0.001\); SLSN-I; lens galaxies coplanar at \(z\approx 0.375\); estimated delays days–weeks.
- **XX** (Ecker, Schweinfurth et al., arXiv:2602.16620): LBT AO J/K imaging; confirms **fifth image E**; SIS/SIE + shear mass models (GLEE + lenstronomy); Einstein-radius masses \(\sim 4.6\times10^{11}\,M_\odot\) (G1) and \(\sim 1.0\times10^{11}\,M_\odot\) (G2).
- Magnification predictions disagree with observed ratios at \(>2\sigma\) — millilensing/microlensing likely.
- Models explicitly **not yet cosmography-grade** (isothermal assumption; profile slope unconstrained).

## Physical intuition

A wine-glass galaxy between us and a distant explosion multiplies the image and delays each path differently. Measure delays + mass model → \(H_0\) from geometry alone, independent of Cepheid ladders and CMB. SLSNe are UV-bright and slow, so at \(z=2\) they remain findable; five images from a binary lens give extra positional locks.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Time delays not yet measured in these papers.
- Microlensing spoils magnification ratios for precision work.
- Mass-sheet / line-of-sight convergence still open.
- Unusual SLSN spectral features (hot, depressed lines) need better phase coverage.
