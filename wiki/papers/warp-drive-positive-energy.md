# Positive-Energy Subluminal Warp Shell (Fuchs et al.)

**One-line summary:** Fuchs et al. (*Classical and Quantum Gravity* 2024) construct a constant-velocity subluminal warp bubble by grafting a tailored shift vector onto a thick, TOV-stabilized matter shell with positive ADM mass, numerically verifying all classical energy conditions without exotic negative energy.

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Jared Fuchs et al. (Applied Physics / collaborators), *Class. Quantum Grav.* **41** (2024) — Warp Factory numerical program.
- Classic Alcubierre problem: FTL (and many subluminal) warp metrics demand negative energy densities and NEC violation.
- Construction: (1) build static spherical shell with Schwarzschild-like exterior asymptotics and positive density; (2) solve TOV for pressure and smooth edges; (3) Lorentz-boost to constant velocity; (4) superimpose a sigmoid-blended shift-vector distribution that circulates momentum inside the shell.
- Outcome: flat interior for passengers; geodesic transport via shift; circulating momentum fluxes; exterior with positive ADM mass.
- Numerical check (Warp Factory): weak, null, strong, and dominant energy conditions held for the constant-velocity solution.
- Observables of the shift: analysis notes Shapiro delay and Lense–Thirring-type precession signatures — not a pure coordinate trick.
- Extends beyond unit-lapse Natário-class restrictions by allowing variable lapse and spatial curvature in the shell.

## Physical intuition

Old warp drives tried to surf a spacetime wave with no real mass budget — free lunch, negative energy. This version builds a **heavy annular battery** first (positive mass shell), then programs a conveyor-belt shift inside the shell so the interior rides along geodesically. Constant cruise only: no free acceleration button yet. Subluminal, not Star Trek FTL — but the energy bookkeeping finally looks like ordinary GR matter.

## Limitations and assumptions

- **Constant velocity only** — start/stop/accelerate phases still likely need exotic or dumped mass/energy.
- Shell mass is enormous (Jupiter-class for $\sim10$ m bubble in typical quotes) — engineering fantasy for now.
- Classical matter only; quantum backreaction / Hawking-like instabilities not included.
- Numerical smoothing choices can hide thin-shell pathologies; independent re-implementations matter.
