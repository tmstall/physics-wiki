- The recoil was measured: the pump pulse doesn't lose photons, it shifts them — a sub-nanometer ultraviolet displacement, revealed by a spectral asymmetry, scaling as the square of the stimulation.
- The radiation stayed perfectly thermal even where the "event horizon" concept breaks down

## Physical intuition

Black holes are predicted to glow faintly — Hawking radiation — and that glow must drain energy from the gravitational field, slowly evaporating the hole. But Hawking's 1974 calculation only describes the glow, never the drain: the "backreaction" of the radiation on its source has remained uncomputed for fifty years, because doing it properly needs a quantum theory of gravity.
