## Physical intuition

Terzan 5 is a dense stellar system in the Milky Way's central bulge, long classified as a globular cluster — the kind of object that's supposed to form in a single burst and never form stars again. Over the past 15 years, evidence has piled up that Terzan 5 breaks this rule, hosting stars with very different chemical compositions.
