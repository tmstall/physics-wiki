**One-line summary:** Guided deep dive from non-renormalizability of GR through black-hole information, Page curve, and holographic encoding — the conceptual bridge from semiclassical paradoxes to bulk reconstruction.

## Key claims and results

- Walks information paradox → Page curve → replica wormholes / island formula lineage.

## Physical intuition

Gravity’s quantum loops need infinitely many new knobs as energy rises — unlike QED’s two knobs. Black holes make that failure personal: Hawking radiation looks thermal, so information seems lost. Modern holography says the radiation *is* a code for the interior after the Page time; islands and wormholes are the geometry of that code. The deep dive is the wiring diagram from “divergences” to “error-correcting bulk.”
