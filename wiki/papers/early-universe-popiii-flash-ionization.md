# Flash Ionization of the Early Universe by Pop III.1 Supermassive Stars

**One-line summary:** Tan (*arXiv* 2025) models a brief $z\sim20$–$25$ “Flash” of ionization from Pop III.1 supermassive stars ($\sim10^5\,M_\odot$ heavy seeds), adding $\tau_{\rm PopIII.1}\sim0.04$ to the CMB optical depth (total $\tau\sim0.10$) while setting the SMBH comoving density at $\sim0.1\,{\rm cMpc}^{-3}$.

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Jonathan C. Tan, “Flash Ionization of the Early Universe by Pop III.1 Supermassive Stars” (arXiv June/Aug 2025 per analysis).
- **Pop III.1:** metal-free supermassive stars in minihalos ($\sim10^6\,M_\odot$), grown with help from dark-matter (WIMP) annihilation heating, ending as $\sim10^5\,M_\odot$ **heavy seeds** for SMBHs.
- Flash epoch: peak ionization at $z_{\rm flash}=20$ or $25$; rise $\sim30$ Myr; decline set by recombination ($\sim29$–$55$ Myr).
- Fiducial ionization fractions $f_{i,{\rm peak}}=0.5$, $f_{i,{\rm vol}}=0.5$ (conservative); $\tau$ scales roughly with $f_{i,{\rm peak}}\times f_{i,{\rm vol}}$.
- Standard galaxy reionization (Robertson-like) $\tau_{\rm gal}\approx0.06$; Pop III.1 adds $\approx0.04$ → total $\tau\approx0.10$ (high-$\tau$ band; above Planck’s $\sim0.054$–$0.063$ preferred window).
- Strömgren/bubble scale: $R_R\sim1\,{\rm cMpc}$ for $S\sim10^{53}\,{\rm s}^{-1}$ ionizing photons and $t_*\sim10$ Myr → $n_{\rm SMBH}\sim0.1\,{\rm cMpc}^{-3}$.
- Free–free radio background from Flash plasma $\sim1$–$3$ K class; can help EDGES-like 21 cm absorption if that signal holds.
- Motivation: observed high-$z$ SMBH abundance + scarcity of intermediate-mass black holes favors heavy seeds over pure light-seed growth.

## Physical intuition

Standard reionization is a slow city-light rollout at $z\lesssim10$. Pop III.1 is a **pre-dawn flash**: a sparse population of super-stars lights megaparsec bubbles, then dies into black-hole seeds, leaving a higher electron column for CMB photons to scatter through. That extra optical depth is a single knob that also hard-codes how many SMBHs you get per cubic megaparsec — one flash, two observables (CMB $\tau$ and seed census).

## Limitations and assumptions

- Idealized uniform Flash; real clumping, Pop III.2, and early AGN add $\tau$ and blur phases.
- Relies on unproven WIMP heating to grow supermassive stars without fragmentation.
- Planck low-$\tau$ preference is a direct tension; LiteBIRD / patchy kSZ / 21 cm (HERA, SKA) will decide.
- Secondary ionization and helium ignored in the simple calculation.
