# A Single Ruthenium Atom That Both Lights the Match and Bends the Bond

**One-line summary:** Single ruthenium atom both initiates and steers a catalytic bond transformation.

## Key claims and results

- XAS (XANES/EXAFS):: X-ray absorption spectroscopy reads the metal's oxidation state and, crucially, counts and measures its nearest neighbors — this is how they claim "Ru–N₄, coordination number ≈ 4" 
- EPR + radical quenching:: electron paramagnetic resonance detects unpaired-electron species (the superoxide radical); adding scavengers that kill specific radicals and watching activity drop is the corrobor
- CO₂-TPD:: CO₂ (an acidic probe molecule) sticks to *basic* surface sites; heating it off and measuring when/how much tells you the site's basicity. This underpins the "Ru–N₄ acts as a base" 
- ELF (from DFT):: the electron localization function maps where electrons are concentrated — used computationally to show the Cβ–H bond getting polarized when the substrate sits on Ru–N₄.
