# ALICE: J/ψ Suppression in Incoherent Photonuclear Production (Gluon Saturation Evidence)

**One-line summary:** ALICE (*PRL* 2026) measures incoherent J/ψ photoproduction in UPC Pb–Pb at $\sqrt{s_{NN}}=5.02$ TeV vs $|t|$; high-$|t|$ cross section is suppressed (ratio $0.52\pm0.13$, ~3σ) relative to low $|t|$, disfavoring a leading-twist shadowing model and better matching gluon-saturation calculations at ~0.2 fm resolution.

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** S. Acharya et al. (ALICE), *Phys. Rev. Lett.* **137**, 052301 (2026), DOI 10.1103/jmwb-75m7; arXiv:2503.18708.
- Setup: ultra-peripheral Pb–Pb (Run 2, 2018); quasi-real photons → J/ψ → $\mu^+\mu^-$; select **incoherent** production (broader $|t|$ than coherent peak).
- $|t|$ acts as spatial resolution knob: highest bin up to $|t|\sim1.44\,{\rm GeV}^2$ → ~0.2 fm class (sub-proton).
- At $W_{\gamma{\rm Pb},n}=633$ GeV: high/low $|t|$ cross-section ratio $0.52\pm0.13$ (~3σ suppression vs low-$|t|$ rise).
- Model comparison: Guzey-style leading-twist shadowing disfavored ≳3–4σ; Cepila saturation ~2–2.5σ off; Mäntysaari saturation ≲2σ — best of the three.
- Framing: **evidence**, not 5σ discovery; single experiment/dataset.

## Physical intuition

Coherent production sees the nucleus as a smooth average; incoherent production sees event-by-event gluon hot spots. Turning up $|t|$ is a finer microscope. At the finest scale, J/ψ rates fall off in a way linear “shadowing without recombination” struggles to match, while saturation models (gluons so dense they recombine) fare better — a work queue hitting its own backpressure.

## Limitations and assumptions

- 3σ evidence class; saturation hints have faded before.
- Closed set of three models, not every non-saturation PDF.
- Saturation and shadowing are related concepts in theory literature — comparison is model-implementation, not two disjoint ontologies.
- Analysis from arXiv extraction; verify bin edges and systematics against primary.
