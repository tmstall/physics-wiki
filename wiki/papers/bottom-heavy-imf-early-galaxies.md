# Bottom-Heavy IMFs Reveal Hidden Stellar Mass in Early Galaxies

**One-line summary:** Ultra-deep VLT+JWST spectroscopy of nine quiescent galaxies at $z\approx0.73$ finds bottom-heavy low-mass IMFs (strongest in the oldest systems), raising inferred stellar masses by up to $\sim4\times$ and amplifying — not relieving — the JWST early-massive-galaxy tension if the IMF is set at formation (*Nat. Astron.* 2026).

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** *Nature Astronomy* (2026), DOI 10.1038/s41550-026-02932-4; arXiv companion 2601.20864.
- Sample: 9 massive quiescent galaxies in the COSMOS Wall at $z\approx0.73$; LEGA-C (VLT) + JWST/IMFERNO NIR spectroscopy; ALF full-spectrum fits (age, abundances, two IMF slopes).
- IMF mismatch $\alpha_{\rm IMF}=(M/L)_{\rm free}/(M/L)_{\rm MW}$ correlates with velocity dispersion / mass / metallicity — same qualitative local trend, now at $z\approx0.7$.
- Oldest systems ($z_{\rm form}\gtrsim5$–6 proxies): most bottom-heavy; abstract reports ~$4\pm1$ mass boost for the oldest galaxy.
- Dynamical (virial) cross-check: MW-IMF stellar masses often approach/exceed dynamical mass; fitted bottom-heavy IMFs reconcile stellar vs dynamical masses for essentially the whole sample (one flagged outlier).
- Implication for JWST $z\sim4$–7 “impossibly early” galaxies: if IMF is formation-set, their standard-IMF masses are underestimates → tension with formation models **worsens**.

## Physical intuition

Bright stars dominate the light; faint dwarfs can dominate the mass. Gravity-sensitive absorption lines (Wing-Ford, Na D, Ca triplet) fingerprint the dwarf/giant mix without resolving stars. At $z\approx0.7$, only combining deep optical + JWST NIR reaches the IMF-sensitive features — then an independent dynamical mass check asks whether the “extra mass” is physical, not a fit artifact.

## Limitations and assumptions

- $N=9$ in one overdensity; not a cosmic average.
- ~20-parameter spectral fits risk age–metallicity–IMF degeneracies; virial check is the main external guardrail.
- High-$z$ JWST link is an inference about presumed descendants, not a direct IMF measurement at $z\sim5$–7.
- Analysis used paraphrase-mediated arXiv fetch — verify contested numerals against the published PDF.
