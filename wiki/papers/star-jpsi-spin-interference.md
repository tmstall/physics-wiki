# Quantum Ghosts in the Collision Zone (STAR J/ψ Spin Interference)

**One-line summary:** STAR sees spin-dependent interference in ultra-peripheral J/ψ photoproduction—two indistinguishable nuclear photon paths act as a polarized double slit, with angular decay patterns flipping sign at the diffractive minimum.

## Key claims and results

- Ultra-peripheral Au+Au: impact parameter large enough that nuclei miss (no hadronic overlap) but exchange quasi-real photons → coherent J/ψ photoproduction off the nuclear gluon field.
- Spatial interference (momentum diffraction from two nuclei as a double slit) was already established; this work evidences **spin/helicity interference** in addition.
- Smoking gun: spin-interference terms in the decay-lepton angular distributions **change sign** at the diffractive $p_T$ minimum — the same null that marks path cancellation in ordinary optics.
- Physics payoff: nuclear gluon tomography with spin-sensitive observables, not only unpolarized gluon averages.

## Physical intuition

Either nucleus can emit the photon that makes the J/ψ—two paths. Paths can also differ in spin orientation. STAR reads that spin double-slit in how the J/ψ’s decay leptons point, locked to the known diffraction null.

## Place on the hard-probe ladder

This is the modern RHIC end of a story that begins with ISR high-$p_T$ jet language ([[high-pt-physics-cern-isr]], [[parton-jets]]): ask what is pointlike and polarized inside nuclear matter. It does **not** replace net-proton cumulants ([[rhic-net-proton-fluctuations]]) or EMC medium PDFs ([[emc-effect-marathon-a3]]) — those constrain fireball fluctuations and cold-nucleus structure functions. UPCs constrain how photons and gluons interfere coherently when the nuclei never touch. Keep the probes separate when stacking on [[nuclear-dense-matter-precision]].

## Limitations and assumptions

- Ultra-peripheral selection purity and detector angular acceptance systematics.
- Model dependence in separating continuum backgrounds and in extracting tomography claims.
