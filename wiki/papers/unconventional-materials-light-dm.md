# Unconventional Materials for Light Dark-Matter Detection (Plasmon Antennas)

**One-line summary:** Target-selection PRL: CDW/demon/doped crystals as low-energy electronic antennas; 100–1000× projected gains vs generic materials; daily modulation from anisotropy; roadmap not discovery (TiSe₂ theory vs data tension noted). PRL DOI 10.1103/jdw6-3556.

## Key claims and results

- DFT-RPA loss functions across the Brillouin zone for three real crystals: TiSe₂ (CDW plasmon ~66 meV), Sr₂RuO₄ (demon mode ~80–800 meV), heavily boron-doped diamond (doping-tunable plasmon).
- Projected kg-year, background-free reach beats superconducting Al and the best Materials Project screen by ~100–1000× for light-mediator scattering and dark-photon absorption.
- Crystal anisotropy → daily rate modulation as Earth rotates relative to the DM wind; strongest for Sr₂RuO₄, smaller for TiSe₂, negligible for cubic HDD.
- Finite-momentum loss functions for Sr₂RuO₄ and HDD are new computational results; TiSe₂ whole-zone response likewise previously missing.
- Small CVT synthesis proof-of-concept for TiSe₂ only; Sr₂RuO₄ / HDD feasibility cites prior growth literature.

## Physical intuition

Light DM deposits tens–hundreds of meV — too soft for nuclear-recoil tanks. A material’s electron energy-loss function is an existing “profiler” for how hard the crystal rings at a given energy and momentum. Pick crystals whose collective modes already sit at that scale (CDW freezes carriers → soft plasmon; multiband out-of-phase “demon”; doping-tuned diamond plasmon) and the same profiler predicts a much louder DM response. Pointy crystals also act as lie detectors: real DM should pulse daily; isotropic backgrounds should not.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Target-selection roadmap, not a detection — every reach curve is an idealized projection.
- TiSe₂ DFT plasmon sits above existing optical/EELS data; phonon features in data are absent from the calculation (authors acknowledge CDW/DFT tension).
- Sr₂RuO₄ and HDD finite-$q$ loss functions have no experimental comparison yet.
- RPA omits excitonic binding; real detectors face IR/thermal and radiogenic backgrounds not in the projections.
- Reach curves quote best single-crystal orientation, not a polycrystalline average.
