# Magnesium-Promoted Benzidine-Type Biaryl Synthesis

**One-line summary:** Substrate design (meta dihalo EWGs + ortho director on nitroarenes) plus aryl Grignards makes a magnesium-promoted [5,5]-sigmatropic benzidine-type rearrangement the main path to regioselective amino-hydroxy biaryls at −45 °C — no transition metals.

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Shumpei Saito et al., *Chem. Eur. J.* (2026). DOI: 10.1002/chem.202503607.
- Classic benzidine [5,5] path resurrected from nitroarene + Grignard space by electronic/steric bias.
- Products: 1-amino-1′-hydroxy-4,4′-biaryls with absolute para–para regioselectivity; broad FG tolerance; ~15 min cryogenic conditions.
- One-pot CF₃-benzimidazole variant via ortho trifluoroacetamide cyclization after the shift.

## Physical intuition

Two benzene rings on a short N–N rope spin in synchrony and clasp hands at the far side ([5,5] dance). Meta halogens are electron sinks that guide the migration terminus; the ortho group locks conformation and blocks competing ortho attack. Magnesium is the cheap Lewis-acidic choreographer — no Pd needed.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Substrate-specific (needs 1,3-dihalo + ortho pattern).
- Mechanism proposed by product/analogy; limited DFT/KIE detail in the analysis.
- Grignard handling at −45 °C still operationally demanding at scale.
