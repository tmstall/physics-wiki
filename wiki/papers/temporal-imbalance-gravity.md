# Temporal Imbalance Theory: Gravity from Time-Flow Gradients

> **Exploratory / non-consensus.** Preprint-level “time-first” gravity proposal. **Not** a community-validated alternative to general relativity. Spectral / Riemann-hypothesis links are conjecture, not derived particle physics.

**One-line summary:** Mullins (Dec 2025 preprint) posits a scalar $\tau$ for local time-flow imbalance, coupled to the metric through a Perelman-style entropy functional $W_\tau$ whose monotone flow yields emergent Newtonian gravity in the weak-field limit and conjectural spectral links to mass spectra.

## Key claims and results

- **Source:** Charles Mullins, “Temporal Imbalance Theory” preprint (December 2025 per analysis).
- Core idea: gravity is not fundamental curvature-from-mass; mass and curvature **emerge** from gradients of a time-imbalance field $\tau(x)$.
- Axioms (analysis summary): observables from $(g,\tau)$ only; monotone entropy flow $W_\tau$ (arrow of time); weak-field recovery of Newtonian gravity with $\tau$ tied to potential; spectral determinant $D_\tau$ with conjectured critical-line rigidity.
- Entropy functional $W_\tau$ mixes scalar curvature, dilaton-like $f$, $|\nabla\tau|^2$, and a potential $V(\tau)$ under a weighted measure — Ricci-flow family with extra $\tau$ terms.
- Metric flow includes $-2\alpha(\nabla_i\tau)(\nabla_j\tau)$ anisotropy and potential “pressure” terms; DeTurck gauge for parabolic well-posedness.
- Weak-field linearization → Poisson equation for $\tau$ sourced by density; test particles accelerate along $\nabla\tau$.
- Speculative extension: spectral zeros of an operator $O_\tau$ conjectured symmetric under $z\to1/z$ with real parts at $1/2$ (Riemann-hypothesis-flavored mass-spectrum story).

## Physical intuition

Think of time as a distributed clock network. Where tick rates disagree, traffic jams form — those jams *are* gravity. Entropy flow is the optimizer that damps chaos into stable solitons (particles, geometries). Newtonian pull is just “follow the clock-rate gradient.” Whether the math actually produces solar-system GR, cosmology, and a particle spectrum is the claim to stress-test — not yet a settled alternative to Einstein.

## Limitations and assumptions

- **Status:** preprint / speculative foundations; not peer-community GR and not a substitute for Einstein equations in applications.
- Strong-field, cosmology, and full PPN solar-system constraints not established in the analysis.
- Spectral / Riemann link is conjecture, not a derivation of particle masses.
- Coupling constants and $V(\tau)$ largely free; predictive power depends on fixing them.
- Operational quantum-clock programs ([[quantum-proper-time-ion-clocks]], [[problem-of-time-cold-atoms]]) do **not** depend on this framework.
- Analysis-based ingest; verify axioms, monotonicity proof, and weak-field limit against the primary preprint before any technical use.
