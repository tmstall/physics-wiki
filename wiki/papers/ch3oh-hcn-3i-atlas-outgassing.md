# Methanol and HCN Outgassing from Interstellar Comet 3I/ATLAS

**One-line summary:** 3I/ATLAS shows very high CH3OH/HCN; methanol and cyanide appear from different nucleus sides (nightside HCN excess); methanol partly from icy grains (≥99% conf. for extra source). Third chemical clue (with CO2-rich JWST, Ni/Fe) of non-Solar-System formation/processing. ApJL DOI 10.3847/2041-8213/ae433b.

## Key claims and results

- ALMA ACA Band 7 maps CH3OH (up to 13 lines) and HCN J=4–3 across six pre-perihelion epochs (r_H ≈ 2.6→1.7 au); models fit directly to visibilities.
- CH3OH/HCN ratios ~124 and ~79 — among the highest in any comet, second only to C/2016 R2; well above the solar-system mean.
- HCN redshifted and antisunward-dominated (up to ~25× on Sept 12); consistent with direct nucleus release (L_p ≈ 0).
- CH3OH blueshifted / sunward-leaning; scale-length sweep excludes pure parent release below L_p ≈ 258 km at 99% confidence — evidence for an extended icy-grain source (mixed origin not ruled out).
- Third independent chemical anomaly after JWST CO2-rich coma and optical Ni/Fe, pointing to non-Solar-System formation or processing.

## Physical intuition

A coma is an expanding TTL cache of ices. Parents write at the nucleus and decay outward; extended sources appear only after grains warm up farther out. Interferometer baselines act like mip levels — short ones see the whole cloud, long ones the compact core — so line shape vs. baseline length reveals where each molecule starts.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Low S/N on longest baselines cannot exclude a residual direct-nucleus CH3OH component alongside the extended source.
- HCN nightside excess may be real nucleus heterogeneity or a rotational-phase projection effect (~16.16 h spin); more epochs needed.
- Two-hemisphere (3D) model is not F-test preferred over simpler 1D+offset at 95% confidence — both remain viable.
- Some epoch kinematics fixed/scaled from the best date rather than independently measured.
- Spatially resolved CH3OH story is single-facility; bulk HCN agrees with JCMT within ~1σ after beam/velocity reconciliation.
