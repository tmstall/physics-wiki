# Primordial Black Holes Are 5D

**One-line summary:** In the Dark Dimension scenario (one extra micron-scale spatial direction), every standard early-universe production channel makes primordial black holes lighter than the Gregory–Laflamme crossover (~$10^{23}$ g), so they are five-dimensional objects; the title is a mass ceiling, not a detection. Anchordoqui, Bedroya & Lüst, *Phys. Rev. D* (2026), DOI 10.1103/g12h-93th, arXiv:2506.14874.

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Luis A. Anchordoqui, Alek Bedroya, Dieter Lüst, *Primordial Black Holes are 5D.* arXiv:2506.14874 v2 (publication version, 3 Oct 2026); *Phys. Rev. D* DOI 10.1103/g12h-93th. Short theory letter: scaling arguments, no simulation, no data.
- Dark Dimension (Montero, Vafa, Valenzuela 2022): one extra spatial loop ~1 micron; Standard Model stuck on a 4D brane; gravity and a few dark fields roam the bulk. Lightest Kaluza–Klein graviton ~0.01–0.1 eV. Crossover mass where a hole’s size equals the extra dimension: $M_{\rm GL} \sim M_{\rm pl}^{2}/M_{\rm KK} \approx 10^{23}$ g (small-asteroid scale). Lighter holes are 5D balls; heavier ones wrap the loop as 4D tubes.
- Formation scan under Swampland constraints: inflationary lumps are set aside (trans-Planckian censorship / Swampland tension); first-order phase transitions hotter than ~TeV produce horizon masses below $M_{\rm GL}$; QCD is a crossover, not a first-order collapse trigger; axion-string loops are capped by the 5D Planck scale, so loop holes sit near ~$10^{12}$ g — about ten orders below the crossover. A late-string loophole below ~10 keV is closed in v2 by axion–photon coupling limits.
- Side result: a hot Standard Model brane above the ~1 GeV “normalcy” temperature needs the extra dimension to grow at the kination speed limit (≲10 e-folds, start ≲20 TeV). The paper also allows no hot brane before ~1 GeV; string loops remain 5D either way.
- Phenomenology: 5D holes evaporate slower (colder, larger surface at fixed mass). Heaviest string-loop holes can live up to ~$10^{13}$ yr if every bound saturates at once; that ceiling, not a typical lifetime, is the KM3NeT-neutrino hook borrowed from a separate Anchordoqui–Halzen–Lüst paper. This letter computes no abundance and no flux.

## Physical intuition

A black hole smaller than the extra dimension does not know the loop is compact. It lives in the bulk as a 5D object: bigger horizon, lower Hawking temperature, longer life than a 4D hole of the same mass. The paper’s job is a packet-size check. The extra dimension is a hard frame-size limit. Walk through every early-universe sender (inflation, phase transitions, string loops) and ask whether any can emit a frame larger than that MTU. Under the scenario’s quantum-gravity bounds, none can.

The asteroid-mass PBH window that 4D studies still treat as open dark matter then becomes the *whole* story: no planetary- or solar-mass primordial holes, and no primordial origin for LIGO–Virgo mergers.

## Limitations and assumptions

- The title overreaches. The result is: *inside the Dark Dimension, after dropping inflationary production, no standard mechanism makes a hole heavier than ~$10^{23}$ g.* If inflation can still make large lumps, 4D holes of any mass return, and the QCD-era argument falls with it. Swampland conjectures are not theorems.
- The paper does not show these holes exist, make up dark matter, or explain KM3NeT. Abundance is left open. The neutrino link is a consistency argument from other papers plus an older extra-dimension shortcut for missing low-energy neutrinos.
- Kination is a *conditional*: the only way to have a hot brane above 1 GeV. Order-one factors in graviton emission and the radion speed limit decide whether even kination is viable; “no hot brane before 1 GeV” is at least as plausible.
- “Lifetimes comparable to the age of the universe” is an upper bound reached only when tension, horizon filling, and max-rate growth all saturate together. A population evaporating today needs an extra, unstated mass-distribution tuning.
- Analyst recomputation: string-loop 5D verdict is robust by a huge margin. Phase-transition verdict is tighter (sits near the line at ~10 TeV). Printed lifetime prefactor in Eq. 22 is off by ≳50 orders of magnitude (typo); the stated 10⁷ GeV evaporation threshold uses the correct value.
