# Hyperon Semileptonic Decays via Polarization and Entanglement (BESIII)

**One-line summary:** BESIII extracts ~3× more observables per Λ→p e ν event using J/ψ→ΛΛ̄ polarization+entanglement; |Vus| depends on f1 theory assumption (0.2194 vs 0.2339) plus a theory-free combination for later reuse; method headline with ~1854 events matching much larger 1980s samples. Nature DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-10818-8.

## Key claims and results

- Double-tagged sample of 1,854 ± 49 Λ → p e⁻ ν̄e events from >10¹⁰ J/ψ decays; first absolute branching fraction $(8.16\pm0.22\pm0.15)\times10^{-4}$.
- Full multi-dimensional angular fit uses production polarization and Λ–Λ̄ entanglement to extract three couplings (g_av, g_w, g_av2) from modest statistics.
- First measurement of weak-electricity coupling g_av2 in this channel; g_w precision comparable to a ~20× larger 1980s Fermilab sample.
- |Vus|_SU(3) = 0.2194 ± …; |Vus|_LQCD = 0.2339 ± 0.0041 — both consistent with CKM unitarity; difference is theory-input, not an experimental split.
- Theory-free product |Vus|·√(f1² + 3g1²) = 0.4496 ± 0.0061 (stat) … for reuse with future form-factor calculations.

## Physical intuition

Kaon and tau |Vus| taps still disagree a bit, so hyperon decays are a third check — but past experiments lacked kinematic handles. J/ψ → ΛΛ̄ hands you polarized, entangled pairs for free; fitting the full angular pattern turns each rare event into several independent coupling measurements instead of one count.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Couplings (especially g_av2, g_w) remain statistics-limited; this is a method foundation, not a final |Vus| settlement.
- Two |Vus| values import different f1(0) assumptions (exact SU(3) vs one lattice calculation) and should not be read as independent cross-checks.
- Hyperon radiative corrections are thinner than for kaon/neutron beta decay.
- Lattice-informed |Vus| leans on a single published f1, g1 calculation; ~2σ tension with older Cabibbo-method hyperon extractions remains open.
- Single-facility (BESIII) technique; independent confirmation needs Belle II / future STCF or PANDA.
