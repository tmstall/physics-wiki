# Topology of the Universe (Review)

**One-line summary:** Nat Astron invited review: topology ≠ curvature; Planck/WMAP nulls constrain less than often claimed (inhomogeneous topologies; loop through us vs smallest loop); next leverage from CMB pol (LiteBIRD/Taurus) + 3D surveys. arXiv:2606.24886.

## Key claims and results

- Geometry (local curvature) and topology (global wraparound) are independent; flat space can still be a 3-torus or one of 17 other Euclidean identifications.
- Non-trivial topology breaks statistical isotropy (and usually homogeneity): CMB $a_{\ell m}$ correlation matrices gain off-diagonal entries that fingerprint the manifold, size, and observer position.
- WMAP / Planck matched-circle and limited Bayesian searches found nothing (e.g. nearest-clone distance ≳ 0.985 × last-scattering diameter) — well-replicated nulls.
- Homogeneity-breaking reframing (COMPACT KL work): searches constrain the loop *through the observer*; the universe’s shortest loop can be up to ~6× smaller (orientable Euclidean; arbitrarily smaller for one special case) or ~2× (non-orientable) — so “ruled out below the horizon” overstates the closed parameter space.
- Detectability via full correlation-matrix / KL methods can persist to observer-loops ~1.3× the last-scattering diameter; next leverage from LiteBIRD / Taurus polarization and multi-redshift 3D surveys.

## Physical intuition

Curvature is the local data type; topology is the addressing scheme. A flat array with wraparound arithmetic still looks locally boring — you only learn about the seam by walking far enough or by noticing aliased correlations. Most real candidate manifolds are not the same from every vantage point, so the loop that runs through Earth can be much longer than the shortest loop anywhere in the manifold. Past satellites mostly constrained our personal loop; a larger slice of topology space remains live than the folk summary suggested.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Review synthesizes the field; the 6× / 2× / 1.3× detectability numbers are recent single-collaboration COMPACT results awaiting independent cross-check.
- Full tensor (polarization) correlation matrices incomplete for some Euclidean classes; S³ work is a renewed effort; H³ remains computationally hard (exponential clone growth).
- Correlation calculations assume standard inflationary eigenmode statistics even though observable topology implies inflation did not run long enough to fully erase pre-inflationary structure — open theoretical gap.
- No first-principles selection rule for which topology (if any) obtains.
- Off-diagonal CMB correlations are typically small vs the diagonal — detection needs aggregation, not a single obvious pattern.
