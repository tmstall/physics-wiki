## Physical intuition

Gravitational-wave astronomy has a gap between ground-based detectors (LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA) and the future space mission LISA — the "mid-band" near 0.1–10 Hz, home to intermediate-mass black-hole mergers and early binary inspirals. Atom interferometers are the leading way to fill it, but they face one dominant obstacle: laser phase noise, which jitters far above the quantum measurement floor.
