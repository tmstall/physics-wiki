# Reading a Supernova’s Onion by Watching It Grow

**One-line summary:** Two Chandra X-ray epochs a decade apart measure proper-motion expansion of remnant G292.0+1.8: outer O/Ne layers expand faster than deep Si/S layers, recovering the explosion’s chemical onion; age is distance-independent (~2.5–4.2 kyr) and the fastest growth sits on the same side as the neutron-star kick.

## Key claims and results

- **Target:** Young oxygen-rich supernova remnant G292.0+1.8 (core-collapse product with a central pulsar).
- Method: align two X-ray images (decade baseline) against catalog reference stars; fit sub-pixel proper motions (~0.1″ class).
- **Chemical stratification still visible in kinematics:** oxygen and neon (outer ejecta shells) expand faster; silicon and sulfur (deeper, nearer the iron core) expand more slowly — the classic onion layers frozen into free expansion.
- **Age from pure angles:** because both size and expansion rate are angular, poorly known distance (~6 kpc class) cancels; dynamical age lands ~2,500–4,200 yr, consistent with pulsar / optical age indicators.
- **Asymmetry:** the shell grows fastest on the **same** side the neutron star was kicked — not the naive “kick opposite fastest ejecta” cartoon — pointing to explosion asymmetry rather than pure momentum bookkeeping.

## Physical intuition

A core-collapse supernova is a pressure cooker that peels: light α-elements outside, silicon-group deeper, iron-group near the core. After the blast, those layers fly outward at different speeds. Ten years later, X-ray “movies” still see outer shells sprinting and inner shells crawling. Align two frames carefully enough and the onion is not just a spectrum — it is a velocity field.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Analysis-based ingest; verify proper-motion magnitudes, element maps, and age window against the primary paper.
- Projection and reverse-shock interactions can re-accelerate or slow layers after free expansion.
- Single remnant; generalization to all CCSNe open.
- Distance still matters for absolute energies even when age is angular.
