# Rotation Was Always the Dark Matter (Alena Tensor)

**One-line summary:** Ogonowski’s Alena Tensor includes rotational (vorticity) energy in the matter stress-energy tensor; that extra source produces an effective \(1/r^2\) “halo” mass density and fits ~80% of SPARC rotation curves with one free parameter—without new particles or modified gravity laws.

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Piotr Ogonowski, *The halo effect and quantum vortices. Not so dark with Alena Tensor*, *Physica Scripta* (2026). DOI: 10.1088/1402-4896/ae59ca.
- Standard dust \(T^{\mu\nu}\) is incomplete for continuous rotating media; full bookkeeping adds vorticity energy.
- Effective gravitational source: rest-mass density **plus twice** the rotational energy density.
- Outer-disk rotational contribution falls as \(1/r^2\)—the classic isothermal-halo profile that flattens rotation curves.
- SPARC fits: one galaxy-dependent anisotropy parameter \(\chi\); weighted RMS competitive with one-parameter MOND/NFW for ~80% of sample.
- Flat-space dual of the same tensor maps to quantum vortices, Mashhoon-type spin–rotation coupling, and Higgs/Yukawa-like vortex-stability conditions (structural, not SM-quantitative).

## Physical intuition

Galaxy “missing mass” can be an accounting error: spin energy is real energy and should gravitate. Treat the disk like a fluid with vorticity (paddle-wheel curl), put that energy into \(T^{\mu\nu}\), and the outskirts already look like a dark halo. The Alena Tensor is a dual dictionary—curved geodesic language ↔ flat four-force language—so the same algebra also talks about quantized vortices.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Solo-author framework; peer review at *Physica Scripta* / *Frontiers*, not yet broad community stress-test.
- Rotation-curve fits alone do not discriminate: no Bullet Cluster, CMB, BAO, or full lensing-map predictions yet.
- Quantum vortex / Higgs analogy is structural; no SM mass or coupling predictions.
- Constant-\(\chi\) per galaxy is a simplification; full \(r\)-dependent tensor not yet applied to clusters with independent lensing.
