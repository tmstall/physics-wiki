# The Horizon Speaks: Direct-Wave Horizon Thermodynamics in GW250114

**One-line summary:** Analysis of GW250114 reports a brief “direct wave” locked near twice the horizon spin frequency and damping with surface gravity—reading Kerr first-law variables from the plunge, distinct from light-ring quasinormal modes.

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** arXiv:2509.09165 (per analysis).
- Direct wave: final-plunge GW component generated near the ergosphere / horizon, not the photon-ring QNM cavity.
- Frequency ~ \(2\Omega_H\); decay set by surface gravity \(\kappa\).
- First claimed direct GW readout of those thermodynamic horizon quantities.
- QNMs still probe exterior light-ring physics—complementary geography.

## Physical intuition

Ringdown is the black hole’s after-ring, sounding from the light-ring “bell.” The direct wave is the companion’s last shout as frame-dragging forces corotation at the horizon itself—closer to the “surface of no return.”

## Limitations and assumptions

- Event-specific SNR and model systematics for separating direct wave vs QNMs.
- Mapping to exact \(\Omega_H,\kappa\) depends on remnant parameters and waveform models.
