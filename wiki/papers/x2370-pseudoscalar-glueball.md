# X(2370): Flavor-Singlet Null Test for the Lightest Pseudoscalar Glueball

**One-line summary:** BESIII’s search in ~10¹⁰ J/ψ events finds no X(2370)→K*(892)⁰K̄⁰ (0.1σ; $\mathcal{B}$ product $<2.7\times10^{-6}$), matching G-parity forbidden decay for a flavor-singlet 0⁻⁺ glueball candidate with small η_c-like mixing (arXiv:2607.20366; adversarial preprint).

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** arXiv:2607.20366 (Jul 2026 preprint). Companion *Nucl. Phys. B* covers other new decay modes — different content.
- Context: X(2370) seen since 2011 in radiative J/ψ; 2024 PWA → $J^{PC}=0^{-+}$ (>9.8σ); mass in lattice glueball window.
- New test: K*(892)⁰K̄⁰ is **G-parity forbidden** for a pure flavor-singlet 0⁻⁺; ordinary non-singlet mesons should populate it.
- Result: significance 0.1σ; 90% CL upper limit $\mathcal{B}[J/\psi\to\gamma X]\times\mathcal{B}[X\to K^*\bar K\to K_S K_S\pi^0]<2.7\times10^{-6}$.
- Interpretation: dominant glueball + 2°–5° mixing with flavor-singlet $c\bar c$ (η_c-like) to fix production rate without reopening the forbidden channel.

## Physical intuition

Gluons carry color, so they can bind to each other — a glueball is a hadron with no valence quarks. Proving “mostly glue” is hard because mixing looks like an ordinary meson. The clever move: pick a decay an ordinary meson would do but a flavor-singlet structurally cannot; silence there is evidence, not just another empty channel.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Single facility (BESIII); no second ~10¹⁰ J/ψ sample elsewhere.
- Preprint; press “50-year search closed” framing stronger than cautious HEP closure.
- Mixing angle from a specific two-state model; purity still theoretically hard.
- Null result within one background model / fixed mass-width; full systematics may live in final journal text.
