## Physical intuition

Breaking a specific chemical bond with light is an old dream: tune a laser to a vibration, pump energy in, climb the vibrational ladder until the bond snaps. The obstacle is **anharmonicity**. Real bonds aren't perfect springs; their vibrational rungs get closer together as you climb (a Morse potential, not a harmonic one). A fixed-frequency laser resonant with the first step, ν=0→1, is already detuned by the second or third step and badly off by the tenth — so it stalls. This is the **anharmonic blockade**.
