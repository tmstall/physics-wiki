# QG Deep Dive 3: Holographic Codes and Bulk Reconstruction

**One-line summary:** Bulk field operators are logical operators of a boundary quantum error-correcting code — islands and horizons as what QEC predicts, not mysterious extras.

## Key claims and results

- Central claim: AdS bulk reconstruction is quantum error correction; a large enough boundary subregion reconstructs bulk operators.
- Island formula answers *that* information returns; QEC answers *how* an exterior observer could decode it.
- Threads: entanglement wedge, complementary recovery, code subspaces.
- Links practical QEC intuition ([[shor-algorithm-budget]] resource language) to gravitational bulk.

## Physical intuition

Store one logical bit in many noisy physical bits; any small erasure is correctable. Holography does the same with geometry: bulk local operators are logical, boundary regions are physical qubits, and black-hole horizons mark where the code’s correctable erasures live. Once you see the code, the island is not magic — it is the recovery region.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Cleanest in AdS; realistic cosmology harder.
- Multi-thread notes, not a single paper digest.
- Toy codes vs continuum gravity still a research gap.
