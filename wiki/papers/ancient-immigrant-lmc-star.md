# The Ancient Immigrant (SDSS J0715−7334)

**One-line summary:** An ultra-metal-poor red giant with \(\lesssim 0.005\%\) solar heavy-element content—chemically cleaner than prior local records and \(\gtrsim 10\times\) cleaner than the most extreme JWST high-\(z\) galaxy candidates—is kinematically assigned to the LMC and matches a single ~30 \(M_\odot\) Population III supernova fingerprint.

## Key claims and results

- Star: SDSS J0715−7334 (“Ancient Immigrant”); informal discovery story via Magellan undergrad night (per analysis / press).
- Metallicity: less than ~1/20,000 solar heavy elements; ~2× more pristine than previous record-holder under standard assumptions; ~40× more iron-poor than prior extreme cases in some comparisons.
- Orbit: halo star today, but trajectory traces to the Large Magellanic Cloud—first ultra-metal-poor star robustly tied to an external galaxy.
- Abundance pattern consistent with enrichment by one Pop III SN (~30 \(M_\odot\)), with caveats on yield-model systematics.
- Exists below the fine-structure cooling metallicity threshold → implies primordial **dust** cooling already operated in early LMC gas.
- Benchmark: JWST “nearly metal-free” galaxies are still far dirtier chemically; bar for claiming true Pop III systems rises sharply.
- arXiv: 2509.21643 (per analysis).

## Physical intuition

This star is a sealed vial of first-generation explosion ash. Its chemistry is a barcode from one ancient supernova; its orbit is a shipping label from the LMC. That it formed at all below the usual gas-cooling floor means dust—not just metal lines—was already helping clouds fragment into low-mass stars early on.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Record margin depends on unmeasured elements (especially oxygen); deeper spectra needed for airtight ranking.
- Pop III yield matches are degenerate across nuclear rates, mixing, and fallback assumptions.
- Dust-cooling argument is inferential (threshold theory ± factor of a few); dust itself not observed.
- Sample of one—population claims need more such stars in more environments.
- Provenance of “independent identification” should be fully clarified in the literature.
