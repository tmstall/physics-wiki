# The Cosmological Constant Is Losing (DESI BAO + Dark Energy)

**One-line summary:** DESI’s multi-tracer BAO map across ~14 million galaxies/quasars (DR2 science; full survey later completed with ~47M objects) prefers evolving dark energy over a pure cosmological constant at roughly 3–4σ when combined with CMB and leading SN Ia datasets—favoring *weakening* dark energy (\(w_a < 0\)).

## Key claims and results

- **Flagship analysis (per ingest):** DESI DR2 Results II — BAO measurements and cosmological constraints (arXiv:2503.14738, *Phys. Rev. D* 2025); April 2026 survey-completion context woven in by the analysis.
- BAO standard ruler measured with BGS, LRG, ELG, QSO, and Lyα forest tracers spanning \(z\sim 0.1\)–4.2.
- Observables: \(D_M/r_d\) (transverse) and \(D_H/r_d\) (radial) vs redshift.
- \(w_0 w_a\)CDM preferred over \(\Lambda\)CDM: present \(w_0\) above −1, \(w_a < 0\) → DE more \(\Lambda\)-like in the past, weaker today.
- Significance depends on SN sample choice; best combinations approach ~4σ class preference for evolution (analysis summary).
- Full 5-year catalog (~47M objects, > original 34M target) feeds 2027-era analyses that can push past discovery threshold or reverse the preference.

## Physical intuition

BAO is a cookie-cutter imprint ~150 Mpc across frozen into the galaxy web. Measuring how large that imprint looks at many redshifts is a tape measure of expansion history. A pure cosmological constant is a fixed throttle forever. DESI’s tape measure prefers a throttle that was stronger in the past and has been easing off—changing both the microphysics of dark energy and the long-term fate of the cosmos (no Big Rip; Big Crunch re-enters the conversation if DE fades enough).

## Limitations and assumptions

- Preference strength is SN-dataset dependent; cross-checks and systematics remain active.
- \(w_0\)–\(w_a\) is a two-parameter ansatz, not a unique microphysical model (quintessence, modified gravity, …).
- Sound-horizon scale \(r_d\) is anchored by early-universe (CMB) physics—shared systematics with \(H_0\) debates.
- Full 5-year cosmology is still forthcoming; DR2 is the current science core for this page.
