# Anyon–Trions Fingerprint Fractional Charges in Twisted MoTe₂

**One-line summary:** Photoluminescence of quantum-confined trions in natural traps of twisted bilayer MoTe₂ reveals red-shifted anyon–trion peaks whose binding energies scale with (fractional charge)² — an optical fingerprint of $e/3$ and $e/5$ anyons in zero-field fractional Chern insulators (Li et al., *Nature* 2026).

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Weijie Li et al., *Nature* (2026), DOI 10.1038/s41586-026-10101-w (analysis framing).
- Twisted MoTe₂ (~3°) hosts fractional Chern insulators (FCIs) at fillings such as $\nu=-2/3$ and $-3/5$ without large external $B$.
- Natural potential traps localize trions; PL spectroscopy maps intensity/energy vs doping, $B$, displacement field, $T$.
- Slight doping past the FCI incompressible fillings produces red-shifted peaks that exist only where parent FCIs live.
- Fan slopes track FCI Chern numbers; electric-field and temperature windows match parent FCIs; intensities anticorrelate with FCI-trion peaks.
- Binding energies ~1.5–2 meV ($-2/3$) and ~0.8–1 meV ($-3/5$) scale roughly as charge² for $e/3$ vs $e/5$ — smoking-gun fractional-charge ratio.

## Physical intuition

Fractional quantum Hall anyons used to need monster magnets and pristine 2DEG. Moiré flat bands fake Landau levels with lattice geometry. Here light, not transport, finds the fractions: a trion (exciton + extra charge) sitting near an FCI edge snags a fractional quasiparticle. The red-shift is the binding energy of that “partial charge packet.” Bigger fractional charge binds tighter ($e/3$ vs $e/5$), so the energy ratio reads the charge fraction without a quantum Hall interferometer.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Traps are uncontrolled bubbles/defects; energies vary across spots.
- Not all fillings show clear anyon–trions; disorder and twist inhomogeneity can fake density plateaus.
- Magnetic fans break pure zero-field conditions for those checks.
- Elementary anyon assumption vs composites still open; braiding not measured.
- Analysis-based ingest; verify binding energies and fillings against primary *Nature* paper.
