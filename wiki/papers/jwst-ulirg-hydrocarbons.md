# JWST Hydrocarbon Factory in ULIRG Nucleus IRAS 07251−0248

**One-line summary:** JWST NIRSpec+MIRI spectroscopy of the eastern compact obscured nucleus in ULIRG IRAS 07251−0248 finds extreme gas-phase small hydrocarbons (including benzene, CH$_3$, polyynes) and deep a-C:H solids; abundances favor cosmic-ray sputtering of grains/PAHs over hot-core chemistry or ice sublimation alone.

## Key claims and results

- **Target:** Eastern nucleus of IRAS 07251−0248, local ULIRG at $z\approx0.08775$; compact obscured nucleus (CON) geometry.
- Instruments: JWST NIRSpec + MIRI/MRS; LTE absorption modeling against mid-IR continuum with covering-factor dilution.
- Gas-phase detections (outflow $\sim160$ km s$^{-1}$, $T_{\rm rot}\sim150$–$250$ K): C$_2$H$_2$, HCN, CH$_4$, **CH$_3$**, C$_4$H$_2$, C$_6$H$_2$, **C$_6$H$_6$ (benzene)** — several claimed as extragalactic firsts for the reactive species.
- Abundances relative to H (log $N_{\rm H}\approx23.3$ cm$^{-2}$) reach $\sim10^{-5}$ for C$_2$H$_2$ and HCN — far above typical Galactic hot cores.
- Solids: deep silicate ($\tau\sim4$), H$_2$O ice, hydrogenated amorphous carbon a-C:H at 6.85/7.25 µm.
- Ruled-out / disfavored drivers: pure high-$T$ gas-phase networks at observed $T$; oxygen depletion; simple ice desorption (CH$_4$/H$_2$O too high vs ice templates).
- Favored driver: **cosmic-ray-induced sputtering** of a-C:H grains and PAH fragmentation; C$_2$H$_2$/H$_2$O correlates with H$_3^+$ equivalent width (CR tracer) across ULIRG sample; $\zeta\sim10^{-14}$ s$^{-1}$ class from H$_3^+$.
- C/O$\sim1$ gas-phase carbon budget; outflows may reseed hydrogenated grains elsewhere.

## Physical intuition

A buried merger nucleus is an industrial cracker: cosmic-ray bullets chew carbon dust and PAH sheets into acetylene, methane, and benzene, then blow the product out at a few hundred km/s. Optical and X-ray eyes see only the dust curtain; JWST’s mid-IR spectrometers read the barcode of the cracker products against the glowing continuum.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Single extreme source; prevalence in the CON population still open.
- Chemical networks still underpredict CH$_4$ by large factors even in C-rich hot models.
- PAH band ratios may sample extended emission not co-spatial with nuclear absorption.
- CR spectrum / AGN contribution assumed rather than fully mapped.
- Analysis-based ingest; verify columns, $\zeta$, and species list against primary paper.
