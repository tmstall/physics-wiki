# Vacuum Birefringence in Magnetar X-rays

**One-line summary:** Magnetar 1E 1547.0−5408 X-rays are 65–82% polarized; a spectral PD dip ~2–4 keV (vacuum resonance) is the QED vacuum-birefringence fingerprint — strongest astrophysical evidence yet, not fully closed (arXiv:2604.10477 lineage / IXPE+NICER+Parkes campaign).

## Key claims and results

- Coordinated campaign on radio-loud magnetar 1E 1547.0−5408: IXPE (2–8 keV polarimetry), NICER (broadband spectrum), Murriyang/Parkes (2.5–4 GHz radio polarimetry), phase-folded to the 2.09 s spin.
- Surface field ~$2.2\times10^{14}\,{\rm G}$ (~5× QED critical field $B_{\rm cr}\approx4.4\times10^{13}\,{\rm G}$).
- Phase-averaged PD ~46±4% (2–8 keV, ~10.5σ); thermally dominated soft band reaches ~**65% at 2 keV**, with phase-resolved peaks ~**82±15%** (2–3 keV) — roughly 3× prior soft-band magnetar IXPE values.
- Energy dependence: PD drops 2→4 keV (~59% in 2–3 keV → ~37% in 3–4 keV), then tentative ~40±11% recovery in 4–8 keV (low S/N). Matches vacuum-resonance mode conversion where plasma and vacuum birefringence cancel.
- Radio RVM: nearly aligned rotator viewed near the pole ($\alpha\sim3^\circ$, $\zeta\sim8^\circ$) — geometry favorable for high coherent PD under VB adiabatic mode tracking out to the polarization-limiting radius.
- Standard condensed-surface / magnetized-atmosphere models struggle above ~40% phase-averaged and under-predict PD during radio-beam (open-field) crossings; VB-inclusive MAGTHOMSCATT radiative-transfer runs fit better.
- Independent reanalyses (Taverna et al. 2026; Li et al. geometric study) **agree on the PD numbers** but argue a broader interpretive window / different preferred geometry — VB not uniquely required on geometry alone.

## Physical intuition

Strong $B$ anisotropizes the vacuum’s virtual $e^+e^-$ response: O- and X-modes acquire different refractive indices (Euler–Heisenberg). Tiny $\Delta n$ still yields enormous adiabatic leverage over magnetospheric path lengths — photon polarization late-binds to the local field out to tens–hundreds of stellar radii, then freezes where the dipole is nearly uniform → high net PD. At the vacuum resonance, plasma and vacuum birefringence cancel, modes mix, and polarization dips — a spectral fingerprint, not just “big $B$ ⇒ polarized.”

## Limitations and assumptions

- VB is inferred from propagation consequences, not a direct $\Delta n$ measurement; other magnetospheric effects can contribute.
- Geometry contested: radio-favored near-pole solution vs X-ray-only more-inclined solutions; VB case strength depends partly on which wins.
- 4–8 keV undersampled; hard nonthermal tail may mix with post-VR recovery.
- Single source, single epoch; five other IXPE magnetars show much lower soft-band PD — geometry matters.
- Strongest evidence yet ≠ closed case; deeper high-energy polarimetry and more radio-loud targets are the near-term falsifiers.
