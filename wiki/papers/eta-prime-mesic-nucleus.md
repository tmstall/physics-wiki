# The Ghostly Nucleus: Semi-Exclusive Hunt for the η′-Mesic Nucleus

**One-line summary:** First semi-exclusive FRS+WASA search for an η′ bound in a carbon remnant sees sub-threshold spectral structures and tightens the η′–nucleus optical potential—but stops short of discovery; coincidence protons kill the quasi-free background that buried the 2016 inclusive try.

## Key claims and results

- **Paper:** Sekiya, Itahashi et al. (η-PRiME / Super-FRS collab.), *Phys. Rev. Lett.* **136**, 142501 (2026). DOI: 10.1103/6vsl-ng7x; arXiv:2509.07824.
- Motivation: η′ mass (~958 MeV) is anomalously large from the U(1)ₐ anomaly / topological vacuum; nuclear density may drop the mass enough to bind (mesic nucleus).
- Setup: 2.5 GeV p on ¹²C; forward deuteron missing-mass via FRS (~0.1% momentum resolution); WASA tags high-momentum protons from one-/two-body η′ absorption.
- Semi-exclusive “conversion” spectrum: require isotropic-ish proton + forward d → suppress quasi-free background by orders of magnitude vs 2016 inclusive PRL.
- Result: tentative sub-threshold structures; fits constrain complex optical potential \((V, W_1, W_2)\) more tightly than before; **not** a discovery claim.
- FAIR-era luminosity is the path to a decisive peak.

## Physical intuition

Most of ordinary mass is vacuum structure, not quark rest mass. The η′ is a fat probe of that topology. Trap it in a nucleus and you ask whether the vacuum “softens” at nuclear density—like measuring refractive index of a material by how much a light pulse slows inside. The signal is a whisper under a jet engine of free η′ production; the trick is to only listen when the ghost also dies with an isotropic proton scream.

## Density probe vs phase-diagram corners

This is a **finite-density hadronic** experiment: nuclear matter at roughly saturation density, not RHIC fireballs and not neutron-star cores. It constrains the complex η′–nucleus optical potential (attraction $V$ vs absorption $W$), which theorists connect to partial U(1)$_A$ restoration. That is a different handle from color superconductivity ([[color-superconductivity-qcd]]), which lives at much higher $\mu_B$ where quark pairing is inevitable by Cooper’s theorem, and from net-proton cumulants ([[rhic-net-proton-fluctuations]]), which walk the hot critical region. On [[nuclear-dense-matter-precision]] this page anchors Thread D — rare probes of vacuum structure in nuclei — without claiming a discovery of an η′-mesic bound state.

## Limitations and assumptions

- Statistics insufficient for discovery; structures are hints.
- Optical-potential fits are model-dependent (real mass shift vs absorptive widths).
- Projection of angular cuts and acceptance systematics.
- Connection to partial chiral restoration is theoretical interpretation, not direct condensate measurement.
