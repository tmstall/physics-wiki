# Fermi-LAT GeV Detection of SLSN 2017egm (Magnetar Engine)

**One-line summary:** Among six nearby superluminous supernovae, only SN 2017egm shows significant Fermi-LAT GeV emission (50–160 days post-explosion, $L_\gamma/L_{\rm opt}\sim1$), matching magnetar wind-nebula leakage once ejecta thins and disfavoring pure circumstellar-interaction powering.

## Key claims and results

- Controlled sample: every SLSN within ~200 Mpc with secure explosion dates during Fermi’s lifetime (six targets).
- Only SN 2017egm (Type I SLSN-Ib, $z\approx0.03$, ~135 Mpc) yields a significant excess (TS ~26 fixed window; ~33 in Bayesian-blocks active window; ~5σ-class pre-trial).
- Active window ~50–160 days post-explosion; soft power-law photon index ~$2.17\pm0.23$.
- Peak $L_\gamma$ comparable to optical peak ($L_\gamma/L_{\rm opt}\approx1$).
- Magnetar wind-nebula models (e.g. Vurm & Metzger 2021 lineage) match timing/luminosity if nebular $\varepsilon_B$ is extremely small or spin-down steeper than dipole $t^{-2}$.
- Pure CSM-collision models fail on timing of shell crossings and predict $L_\gamma/L_{\rm opt}\ll10^{-2}$.
- Late optical bumps after day ~200 still need hybrid/precessing-disk extras even in the magnetar picture.
- CTAO forecasts: 2017egm-like events detectable to ~140 Mpc in magnetar channel, not in CSM (pair absorption).

## Physical intuition

SLSNe are too bright and long-lived for ordinary radioactive nickel alone. Two engines compete: **ram a dense shell** (CSM) or **keep a flywheel dynamo spinning** (millisecond magnetar wind). Gamma rays are the tell: a buried central accelerator only leaks GeV photons after the ejecta garage door lifts. Fermi caught that leak once — at the right epoch and with a gamma-to-optical ratio that pure shell collisions almost never deliver.

## Limitations and assumptions

- $N=1$ detection; cannot yet say magnetars dominate all SLSNe.
- Late-time Fermi limits too shallow to map full spin-down decay.
- Source-confusion formally possible though multiwavelength monitoring disfavors.
- Model tuning adjusted to this event; CTAO forecasts inherit that.
- Analysis-based ingest; verify TS, windows, and luminosity ratios against primary paper.
