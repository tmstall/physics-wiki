## Physical intuition

Molecules with more than one stable geometric arrangement (isomers) sitting close in energy are common, but watching — let alone controlling — the population balance between them in the gas phase is hard. Ions parked in ultracold helium nanodroplets (0.37 K) are a favorite platform for this: the droplet acts as a gentle refrigerant, absorbing the heat released after each IR photon is absorbed so the ion returns to its vibrational ground state and can absorb again.
