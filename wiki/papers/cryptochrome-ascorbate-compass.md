## Physical intuition

The radical-pair compass is the leading explanation for how migratory birds see the Earth's field. Blue light hits the FAD cofactor buried in cryptochrome; FAD grabs an electron down the tryptophan chain, leaving two unpaired electrons — one on the flavin (FAD•−), one on a tryptophan (TrpH•+) — separated by a couple of nanometers. Those two electron spins are born spin-correlated (a pure singlet) and then evolve coherently. Tiny magnetic interactions — hyperfine coupling to nearby H and N nuclei, plus the ~50 µT geomagnetic Zeeman term — interconvert singlet and triplet, and singlet vs. triplet recombine to *different* chemical products.
