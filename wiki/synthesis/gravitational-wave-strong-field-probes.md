# Gravitational Waves as Strong-Field Probes

**One-line summary:** This wiki’s GW pages form a comparative map of **what a waveform (or timing residual) can read** — horizon thermodynamics, remnant heuristics, siren cosmology, strain gauges, beyond-GR merger templates, and binary-pulsar strong-field tests — distinct from semiclassical evaporation endpoints and from AGN fuel–feedback loops.

## Why pull these together

Horizon “direct waves,” entropy-max remnant spins, second-order strain gauges, GW170817 sirens, EFT merger corrections, and young binary pulsars sit in different catalogs. In this wiki they answer one instrument question:

> What **physical quantity** does the gravitational-wave channel actually unlock, and what must stay in another synthesis?

No single event owns Kerr thermodynamics, $H_0$, and beyond-GR ringdown. Stack them and four probe classes appear: (1) **horizon / remnant thermophysics from waveforms**, (2) **what freely falling observers measure**, (3) **multimessenger and timing laboratories**, (4) **production / early-universe GW neighbors** (explicitly not detectors). The tensions live where “the horizon speaks” is an event-specific claim, or where a siren $H_0$ is still $N=1$.

This synthesis is **GW as a strong-field instrument**. Semiclassical evaporation, NEC violation, and regular Penrose endpoints live on [[black-hole-evaporation-energy-conditions]]. AGN duals, winds, and cavities live on [[black-hole-feedback-and-changing-look-agn]]. Expansion-history use of sirens also appears on [[cosmology-expansion-history-and-structure]]. Speculative GW bound-state constructions live lightly on [[modified-speculative-gravity]].

## Thread A — Horizon and remnant thermophysics from waveforms

**Papers:** [[horizon-direct-wave-gw250114]], [[entropy-maximization-bh-mergers]], [[qg-deep-dive-1-mergers-emission]]  
**Concepts:** [[black-hole-thermodynamics]]

### A1 — Direct wave vs quasinormal ringdown

[[horizon-direct-wave-gw250114]]: GW250114 analysis reports a brief plunge component locked near $2\Omega_H$ and damping with surface gravity $\kappa$ — a claimed direct GW readout of Kerr first-law variables, geographically closer to the horizon than light-ring QNMs.

**Intuition:** QNMs are the black hole’s after-ring from the photon-orbit bell. The direct wave is the companion’s last shout as frame-dragging forces corotation at the horizon itself.

**Limits:** Event-specific; separating direct wave from QNMs is model-dependent.

### A2 — Entropy maximization as remnant heuristic

[[entropy-maximization-bh-mergers]]: map instantaneous binary $(M,J)$ to a Kerr entropy and maximize → predicts remnant spin without full NR. Useful as a **thermodynamic bookmark**, not a replacement waveform family.

### A3 — Beyond-GR merger emission (EFT deep dive)

[[qg-deep-dive-1-mergers-emission]]: analytical EFT / large-mass-ratio framework for how merger and ringdown change when GR is deformed — the “what would a template look for?” cousin of Thread A’s GR thermophysics.

**A vs evaporation synthesis:** Evaporation pages ask whether interiors stay regular under Hawking flux. This thread asks what **ringdown and plunge already encode** for astrophysical remnants. Shared concept [[black-hole-thermodynamics]]; different experiment.

**Papers:** [[gw170817-jet-hubble]], [[psr-j1906-binary-timing]]

- [[gw170817-jet-hubble]]: only named-host GW event for siren $H_0$; jet geometry reduces inclination systematics. Dual citizenship with [[cosmology-expansion-history-and-structure]].
