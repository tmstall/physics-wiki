# Modified and Speculative Gravity Approaches

**One-line summary:** This wiki’s pages that alter or re-found gravity — massive gravitons, positive-energy warp shells, higher-dimensional classical models, time-first entropy flows, entropy-as-compression, MOND-like force laws — form a comparative map of **what each approach actually modifies** and which **constraints already live in the wiki**, without crowning a successor to general relativity.

> **Disclaimer (read first).** This synthesis is a **catalog of approaches**, not an endorsement. Several core pages are marked **exploratory / non-consensus**. Peer-community GR, multimessenger GW tests, solar-system constraints, and energy-condition bookkeeping remain the default. Do not treat dRGT, warp metrics, 5D classical models, or temporal-imbalance theory as established replacements for Einstein gravity.

## Why pull these together

Modified gravity, exotic metrics, and “gravity emerges from X” programs usually sit in separate corners. In this wiki they answer one systems question:

> What piece of the standard package does each approach **change** — the graviton mass, the energy budget of a metric ansatz, the ontology of spacetime, or the force law — and what **wiki-local constraints** already stress-test that change?

No winner. Stack them and four layers appear: (1) **field-theory modifications** still written in a GR-like language (massive gravity), (2) **metric engineering** inside classical GR (warp shells, gravastars), (3) **foundational rewrites** that claim to derive gravity (5D classical, temporal imbalance, gravity-from-entropy), (4) **phenomenological force-law alternatives** (MOND / EFE) that target galaxy data rather than horizons. The tensions live where “works” means ghost-free Lagrangian, numerical energy conditions, or rotation-curve fit.

This synthesis is **modified / speculative gravity**. Semiclassical black-hole evaporation and energy conditions in *standard* GR live on [[black-hole-evaporation-energy-conditions]]. That page is a **constraint library** for this one, not a competing “theory of everything.”

## Thread A — Field-theory modifications (still “gravity as a field”)

**Papers:** [[massive-gravity-drgt]], [[universe-gas-pedal-leaky]], [[desi-evolving-dark-energy]] · concepts [[dark-energy-equation-of-state]], [[hubble-tension]]

### A1 — Ghost-free massive gravity (dRGT)

[[massive-gravity-drgt]]: de Rham–Gabadadze–Tolley nonlinear massive gravity gives the graviton a tiny mass while killing Boulware–Deser ghosts. Vainshtein screening protects local GR. Cosmological pitch: finite range can drive late acceleration **without** a separate dark-energy fluid in some branches. One-loop studies (e.g. arXiv:1307.7169 lineage) argue the ghost-free structure can survive quantum corrections below the Planck scale in studied regimes.

**What it modifies:** graviton mass spectrum and IR force law — **not** the claim that gravity is a spin-2 field theory.

**Constraints already in the wiki:**
- Multimessenger / GW speed ≈ $c$ and solar-system tests force tiny $m$ and healthy screening (page limitations).
- DESI evolving-$w$ preference ([[desi-evolving-dark-energy]], [[dark-energy-equation-of-state]]) is a **data-driven DE narrative**, not a confirmation of massive gravity. Treat dRGT as a **competing** IR story, not the DESI fit.
- Source for the wiki page is profile + pointers — verify against primary dRGT literature before technical use.

**Intuition:** Massless gravity is infinite-range radio. Massive gravity is a transmitter with a horizon. Locally you sit inside the Vainshtein bubble; cosmically the band snaps.

### A2 — Neighboring acceleration narratives

[[universe-gas-pedal-leaky]] and DESI pages discuss cosmic acceleration dynamics and evolving DE. They constrain **what any IR modification must reproduce**, whether the microphysics is a fluid $w(z)$ or a massive graviton.

**A-thread status:** Research-program class — serious literature, open viability windows, **not** a replacement of GR in applications.

## Thread B — Metric engineering inside (or next to) classical GR

**Papers:** [[warp-drive-positive-energy]], [[gravastar-dust-collapse]], [[black-hole-third-law-violation]] · concept [[null-energy-condition]] · synthesis [[black-hole-evaporation-energy-conditions]]

### B1 — Positive-energy subluminal warp shell

[[warp-drive-positive-energy]] (Fuchs et al., *CQG* 2024): thick TOV-like matter shell + tailored shift vector → constant-velocity subluminal bubble with flat interior, positive ADM mass, and **claimed satisfaction of all classical energy conditions** in Warp Factory numerics.

**What it modifies:** the metric ansatz and stress-energy distribution for a “warp” bubble — still classical GR bookkeeping, **not** a new fundamental field theory.

**Constraints:**
- Classic Alcubierre needs exotic NEC-violating matter; this construction’s win is **bookkeeping**, not FTL.
- **Constant velocity only** — acceleration phases likely reintroduce exotic requirements.
- Jupiter-scale mass for ~10 m bubbles (typical quotes) → not engineering-ready.
- Independent re-implementation and quantum backreaction open.

### B2 — Energy conditions as the shared referee

[[null-energy-condition]] and [[black-hole-evaporation-energy-conditions]]: singularity theorems assume NEC-like conditions; Hawking flux violates NEC semiclassically and enables regular evaporation endpoints in some models ([[evaporating-charged-black-holes]]). Warp history runs the opposite direction — *demanding* NEC violation for FTL.

**B-thread tension:** Semiclassical evaporation **needs** NEC violation to open regular endpoints. Warp engineering **fights** to restore energy conditions for a cruise metric. Same concept hub, opposite design goals. Neither licenses unconstrained exotic matter as free lunch.

**Related GR constructions:** [[gravastar-dust-collapse]] (horizonless mimickers), [[black-hole-third-law-violation]] (third-law counterexamples in vacuum 5D) — stress-test classical lore without rewriting the Einstein–Hilbert action.

## Thread C — Foundational rewrites (exploratory)

**Papers:** [[five-dimensional-classical-gravity]], [[temporal-imbalance-gravity]], [[gravity-from-entropy]], [[retrocausal-noisy-channel-capacity]] · concepts [[quantum-proper-time]], [[loop-quantum-cosmology]]

> **Thread-wide status:** These pages explore **ontology** (what gravity *is*), not incremental parameter fits. Treat claims as **proposals under discussion**. Lab quantum-clock programs and standard GR applications **do not** depend on them.

### C1 — Classical 5D worldlines

[[five-dimensional-classical-gravity]] (Strubbe, *Sci. Rep.* 2025): classical 5D manifold with evolution parameter $\tau$; worldline density sources GR-like equilibria; zigzag paths and multi-worldline bundles **claimed** to emulate entanglement and interference without quantizing the metric.

**What it modifies:** dimension count + dynamics ontology; aims to *derive* quantum-like phenomena classically.

**Constraints:** No fermions/gauge sector; weak-field numerics only; QM equivalence unproven; page already carries exploratory blockquote.

### C2 — Temporal imbalance (time-first gravity)

[[temporal-imbalance-gravity]] (Mullins preprint): scalar $\tau$ for local time-flow imbalance; Perelman-style entropy functional $W_\tau$; gravity as emergent from $\nabla\tau$; spectral/Riemann links conjectural.

**What it modifies:** primacy of time-flow over mass→curvature; claims Newtonian limit from $\tau$ gradients.

**Constraints:** Not peer-community GR; free couplings; no full PPN/cosmology suite on disk; **explicitly** disconnected from operational [[quantum-proper-time-ion-clocks]] / [[problem-of-time-cold-atoms]] programs.

### C3 — Gravity from entropy / compression

[[gravity-from-entropy]]: thermodynamic / information-compression framing of gravity (analysis-level). Shares language with [[black-hole-thermodynamics]] but is not a drop-in replacement field equation set used in precision tests.

### C4 — Neighbor: topological protection of $\Lambda$

[[topological-cosmological-constant]] / [[gravitational-theta-vacua]]: quantize/protect $\Lambda$ via gravitational $\theta$-sectors — modifies the **stability story** of the cosmological term, not the large-scale force law like massive gravity, and does not by itself select the observed tiny value.

**C-thread tension with A/B:** A and B still speak field equations and energy conditions. C often changes **what counts as fundamental**. Keep the layers separate when citing.

## Thread D — Phenomenological force-law alternatives

**Papers:** [[mond-external-field-sparc]], [[newton-ksz-force-law]], [[alena-tensor-rotation-dm]] · concept [[modified-newtonian-dynamics]]

[[mond-external-field-sparc]]: SPARC rotation curves with free external-field parameter compared to environmental Newtonian field → EFE-consistent fits and claimed SEP tension with standard GR+DM assumptions.

[[newton-ksz-force-law]]: kSZ pairwise velocities as a force-law test on megaparsec scales ($n\approx2.1$ class in analysis).

**What they modify:** weak-field / low-acceleration dynamics and large-scale force-law checks — **not** graviton mass or warp metrics.

**Constraints:** Live next to DM and structure pages; do not automatically unify with dRGT or warp shells. Galaxy-scale phenomenology and IR massive gravity can both “fix acceleration,” but they are different mechanisms with different falsifiers.

**D vs A:** MOND/EFE targets **galactic** data. Massive gravity targets **cosmic IR** and GW multimessenger windows. Same slogan (“modify gravity”), different regimes.
