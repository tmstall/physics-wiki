# Dark Matter Detection Channels

**One-line summary:** This wiki’s dark-matter pages form a comparative map of **search and bound channels** — quantum optics of axion cavities, cosmological heating/glow limits, indirect γ-ray lines, dynamical streams, and GW freeze-in production — not a MOND-vs-particles ontology fight and not a PeVatron messenger catalog.

## Why pull these together

Axion haloscope theory, PBH γ-glow bounds, dark-photon plasma saturation, cluster γ-line claims, Oyashio streams, and GW freeze-in sit scattered across HEA and modified-gravity overflow. In this wiki they answer one sensor question:

> What **measurable** would light up (or go dark) if dark matter were this candidate, and what detector physics can **erase** an otherwise exotic signature?

No single paper owns laboratory bosonic DM, evaporating PBHs, and extragalactic streams. Stack them and five channel classes appear: (1) **lab / quantum-optical sensors**, (2) **cosmological heating and glow bounds**, (3) **indirect γ-ray spectral lines**, (4) **dynamical mass tracers**, (5) **production mechanisms** (explicitly not searches). A thin **contrast row** points at force-law alternatives without absorbing [[modified-speculative-gravity]].

This synthesis is **detection and bound channels**. Expansion history and LSS phenomenology live on [[cosmology-expansion-history-and-structure]]. Speculative gravity / MOND / Alena live on [[modified-speculative-gravity]]. PeVatrons and multi-messenger engines live on [[high-energy-astrophysics-multimessenger]]. PBH *interiors* after a horizon exists live on [[black-hole-evaporation-energy-conditions]]; PBH *origin* on [[primordial-black-holes]] / [[pre-bang-leftovers]].

## Thread A — Laboratory / quantum-optical sensors

**Paper:** [[axion-detector-quantum-erasure]]

[[axion-detector-quantum-erasure]]: fully quantum axion–photon conversion in cavity haloscopes. Mode averaging (quantum CLT) plus absurdly small conversion efficiency erase intrinsically nonclassical $P$-function signatures before readout. Classical oscillating-field forecasts are what the quantum theory predicts under realistic detector coarse-graining — not a lazy approximation.

**Intuition:** Even if the galactic axion field were a Schrödinger cat, the cavity still reports a classical noisy tone.

**Limits:** Detailed for resonant cavities; qualitative for ABRACADABRA / CASPEr / dish; engineered galactic-scale entanglement could evade CLT (no known production channel). Does **not** kill classical spectral anomalies.

**Removed (2026-08-18):** Former page `synchrotron-dm-detector` was a **mis-titled ingest** (Claude-export clickbait title). Rewritten as [[bessy-tes-soft-xray-spectrometer]] — BESSY II soft x-ray TES instrumentation for materials, **not** a DM channel. Lives on [[ultrafast-optics-and-solid-state-emitters]].

## Thread B — Cosmological heating and glow bounds

**Papers:** [[gamma-glow-pbh-detector]], [[dark-photon-plasma-saturation]]  
**Concepts:** [[primordial-black-holes]]

### B1 — γ-glow as PBH detector

[[gamma-glow-pbh-detector]]: evaporating asteroid-mass PBHs contribute to the extragalactic γ-ray glow; updated bookkeeping (including positron / invisible channels) **tightens or closes** the low-mass PBH DM window (analysis-level: e.g. $10^{14}$ g cannot be more than a tiny DM fraction).

### B2 — Dark-photon resonance self-limits

[[dark-photon-plasma-saturation]]: resonant dark-photon → Langmuir conversion saturates via ponderomotive detuning and wave breakup; deposited energy ~$O(1)$–$O(100)\times$ thermal, not the linear dump. Classic resonant $\varepsilon$ bounds weaken by ~$10^3$–$10^7$; non-resonant / dark-ages / Lyα channels become leading.

**B-thread lesson:** A null (or glow) result is only as strong as the **plasma and emission model**. Nonlinear saturation and missing channels rewrite the bound, not necessarily the candidate’s existence.

**Handoff:** Early thermal history messengers that are *not* DM bounds stay on [[high-energy-astrophysics-multimessenger]] and [[cosmology-expansion-history-and-structure]].

## Thread B′ — Indirect γ-ray spectral lines

**Paper:** [[43gev-gamma-ray-line-clusters]]  
**Instrument cousins (not DM claims):** [[magnetar-slsn-2017egm-fermi]], [[peters-cycle-cosmic-rays]]

[[43gev-gamma-ray-line-clusters]]: 15.5 yr Fermi-LAT Pass 8 excess at ~43 GeV toward Virgo / Fornax / Ophiuchus (global ~$4.3\sigma$ after look-elsewhere). Survives Earth-limb, incidence-angle, continuum-shape, width, and ROI-size checks framed against the 2012 ~130 GeV false alarm. Canonical velocity-independent annihilation is **disfavored** by a Galactic-Center non-detection; Sommerfeld / velocity-dependent DM or unidentified astrophysics remain open. Sub-$5\sigma$, single-group longitudinal claim — VLAST (~2 yr) is the named near-term falsifier.

**Intuition:** Diffuse γ glow ([[gamma-glow-pbh-detector]]) integrates continuum exhaust. A **line** bets on monochromatic two-body kinematics. Same telescope family as SLSN GeV leaks ([[magnetar-slsn-2017egm-fermi]]), different science: engine timing vs DM spectral spike.

**Limits:** Below discovery threshold; not an independent-pipeline replication; $J$-factor / NFW slope systematics move quantitative cross-section reads even if the bump is real.

## Thread C — Dynamical tracers


**Paper:** [[oyashio-extragalactic-gc-stream]]

First confirmed extragalactic globular-cluster stellar stream (Oyashio in UGC 9050-Dw1) used as a generative-model probe of host halo mass and inner slope — stream morphology as a DM well tracer outside the Milky Way.

**Intuition:** MW streams are resolved-star system traces. Oyashio is the same idea as an unresolved light smear 35 Mly away.

**Limits:** Photometric stream detection ≫ significance of DM parameter posteriors; single galaxy / single modeling family.

**Optional cousins (not core):** [[pulsars-satellite-masses]] for dynamical mass language.
