# High-Energy Astrophysics, Multi-Messenger Signals & Early-Universe Thermal History

**One-line summary:** This wiki’s energetic-transient, multi-messenger, ISM-chemistry, and reionization pages form a comparative map of **messengers and epochs** — gamma rays from buried engines, radioisotope ash in ice, Lyα-lit filaments, CR-driven hydrocarbon factories, Pop III.1 flash ionization, and local accretion outbursts — that connect how the universe heats, ionizes, enriches, and lights itself.

## Why pull these together

High-energy astrophysics, multi-messenger astronomy, and early-universe thermal history look like separate catalogs: Fermi SLSNe, Antarctic $^{60}$Fe, MUSE filaments, JWST ULIRGs, Pop III theory, FUor tantrums. In this wiki they answer one systems question:

> Which **messenger** carries information about which **epoch**, and which **energy channel** (radiation, particles, chemistry, ionization) is doing the work?

No single paper owns the whole ladder. Stack them and four channels appear: (1) **prompt high-energy photons** from engines, (2) **nucleosynthetic fossils** in the local ISM and ice, (3) **line emission and absorption** that light or process gas across cosmic time, (4) **ionization history** from first stars to the CMB. The tensions live where “signal” means a burst, a chemical barcode, or an integrated electron column.

This synthesis is **astrophysical messengers and thermal history**. SMBH fuel–feedback loops live on [[black-hole-feedback-and-changing-look-agn]]. Condensed-matter topology lives on [[condensed-matter-topology-fractionalization]]. Do not collapse those programs into this page.

## Thread A — Energetic engines and high-energy photons

**Papers:** [[magnetar-slsn-2017egm-fermi]], [[aquila-booster-pevatron]], [[lab-blazar-pair-instability]], [[supernova-onion-expansion]], [[gamma-glow-pbh-detector]], [[dense-plasma-opacity-revision]]

### A1 — SLSN GeV leak as engine discriminator

[[magnetar-slsn-2017egm-fermi]]: among six nearby SLSNe with secure dates in Fermi’s lifetime, only SN 2017egm shows significant GeV emission (~50–160 days, $L_\gamma/L_{\rm opt}\sim1$). Timing and luminosity favor a magnetar wind-nebula leaking once ejecta thin, over pure CSM-shell collisions.

**Intuition:** Two engines compete — ram a dense shell, or keep a millisecond flywheel spinning. Gamma rays are the tell: a buried accelerator only shines after the ejecta garage door lifts.

**Limits:** $N=1$ detection; late-time Fermi shallow; CTAO forecasts model-tuned.

### A2 — Galactic PeVatrons and CR composition

[[aquila-booster-pevatron]], [[peters-cycle-cosmic-rays]], [[beyond-iron-ultraheavy-cosmic-rays]]: Galactic accelerators and multi-species CR spectra map where the knee-scale machine lives and what nuclei it launches. [[supernova-onion-expansion]] supplies the layered ejecta context that $^{60}$Fe ash later samples.

### A3 — Blazar cascades and lab IGMF physics

[[lab-blazar-pair-instability]]: CERN pair beams through plasma show Weibel-like instabilities suppress under realistic divergence — so missing cascade GeV from blazars still favors primordial IGMF over pure plasma short-circuit.

**A-thread tension:** Transient GeV (SLSN) diagnoses a **stellar engine**. PeVatron/CR pages diagnose **Galactic acceleration**. Lab blazar work diagnoses **intergalactic B**. Same “high-energy photon” language, three different objects.

## Thread B — Nucleosynthetic fossils and local multi-messenger

**Papers:** [[ice-core-fe60-local-cloud]], [[supernova-onion-expansion]], [[beyond-iron-ultraheavy-cosmic-rays]], [[interstellar-glaciers-spherex]]

[[ice-core-fe60-local-cloud]]: EPICA ice (40–80 kyr BP) shows lower $^{60}$Fe flux than modern snow — the Sun entered a richer patch of the Local Interstellar Cloud tens of kyr ago; modern $^{60}$Fe is still SN ash carried by the cloud, not a fresh local blast.

**Intuition:** $^{60}$Fe is a still-smoldering fuse from core-collapse nucleosynthesis. Antarctic ice is a strip chart of windshield collection. Time variation maps LIC internal structure along the Solar trajectory.

**Bridge to Thread A:** The same supernova physics that lights GeV engines and peels onion ejecta ([[supernova-onion-expansion]], [[magnetar-slsn-2017egm-fermi]]) leaves radioisotope ash you can dig out of ice. Multi-messenger does not require a simultaneous GW+γ coincidence — it can be **geological** and **isotopic**.

**Limits:** One core/site; tiny atom counts; which SN remains open; Beyond EPICA for pre-entry baseline.

## Thread C — Gas lighting, chemistry, and accretion across the web

**Papers:** [[muse-quasar-filament-z3]], [[jwst-ulirg-hydrocarbons]], [[iras-21204-fuor]], [[interstellar-glaciers-spherex]], [[interstellar-sulfur-ice]], [[dr21-magnetic-accretion]], [[cosmos-web-cosmic-web]] · concept [[cosmic-web]]

### C1 — Filaments as density labs (cosmic noon)

[[muse-quasar-filament-z3]]: 142 h MUSE maps a Lyα filament between a $z\approx3.22$ quasar pair (~250 pkpc bridge); CGM→IGM break near the virial radius; densities within ~factor 2–3 of IllustrisTNG analogs.

**Intuition:** Quasars are floodlights. Lyα paints the mist. You watch the steep city-limits (CGM) hand off to the flatter interstate (IGM).

### C2 — Buried nuclei as hydrocarbon crackers

[[jwst-ulirg-hydrocarbons]]: JWST mid-IR finds extreme gas-phase small hydrocarbons (incl. benzene, CH$_3$) in a ULIRG CON; abundances favor **cosmic-ray sputtering** of a-C:H grains/PAHs over pure hot-core or ice sublimation.

**Intuition:** Dust curtain hides the factory; mid-IR reads the barcode. CR bullets crack PAHs into acetylene and benzene, then outflows reseed grains.

### C3 — Local stellar gulps and ice fuel pipes

[[iras-21204-fuor]]: low-mass YSO eruption ($\gtrsim5$–$6$ mag, $\dot{M}\gtrsim3\times10^{-5}\,M_\odot\,{\rm yr}^{-1}$) with P Cygni winds, polarization, energy-dependent lines, and a 1948 prior flare.

[[interstellar-glaciers-spherex]], [[interstellar-sulfur-ice]], [[dr21-magnetic-accretion]]: ice inventories and magnetic ridge accretion supply the cold chemistry and plumbing that feed star formation — the local end of the cosmic-web fuel line ([[cosmic-web]], [[cosmos-web-cosmic-web]]).

**C-thread tension:** MUSE filaments are **IGM/CGM structure** at $z\sim3$. ULIRG hydrocarbons are **extreme nuclear chemistry**. FUors are **pc-scale disk instability**. All process gas and light; only the first is a large-scale-structure probe in the usual sense.

**Overlap with BH synthesis:** Filaments → CND feeding and quasar winds already live on [[black-hole-feedback-and-changing-look-agn]]. This page keeps the **messenger and chemistry** angle; that page keeps the **AGN control loop**.

## Thread D — Early ionization and thermal history

**Papers:** [[early-universe-popiii-flash-ionization]], [[muse-quasar-filament-z3]], [[euclid-high-z-quasar-census]], [[desi-evolving-dark-energy]], [[hubble-tension]]

[[early-universe-popiii-flash-ionization]]: Pop III.1 supermassive stars (~$10^5\,M_\odot$) at $z\sim20$–$25$ flash-ionize ~cMpc bubbles, adding $\tau\sim0.04$ (total $\tau\sim0.10$) and planting heavy SMBH seeds at ~$0.1\,{\rm cMpc}^{-3}$.

**Intuition:** Standard reionization is a slow city-light rollout at $z\lesssim10$. Pop III.1 is pre-dawn flash: super-stars light bubbles, die into seeds, leave extra electron fog for CMB photons. One knob, two observables (CMB $\tau$ and seed census).

**Downstream links:** High-$z$ quasar census and duals ([[euclid-high-z-quasar-census]], [[high-z-quasar-pair-merger]]) test whether heavy seeds are needed. Lyα filaments at $z\sim3$ ([[muse-quasar-filament-z3]]) show later web gas after reionization is largely done. Parameter tensions ([[hubble-tension]], [[desi-evolving-dark-energy]]) can feel a high-$\tau$ lever — speculative, not settled.

**Limits:** Idealized flash; WIMP heating unproven; Planck prefers lower $\tau$; 21 cm / LiteBIRD / patchy kSZ will decide.

**D vs A tension:** Pop III.1 is **theory-first thermal history**. Fermi SLSN is **data-first engine physics**. Both use “high energy” language; only one is multi-messenger in the instrument sense.
