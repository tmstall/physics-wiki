# SMBH–Star Encounters: Orbits, Spins, and Repeating Disruptions

**One-line summary:** This wiki’s Galactic-Center and repeating-partial-TDE pages form a comparative map of **how stars probe or feed supermassive black holes** — extreme S-star orbits that unlock spin sensitivity, and Hills-capture pre-spin that lets stripped stars dim across many flares — separate from AGN wind/feedback loops and from GW waveform instruments.

## Why pull these together

S301 around Sgr A* and hydro models of repeating partial TDEs look like different catalogs (interferometric astrometry vs SPH flares). In this wiki they answer one systems question:

> What does a **single star** tell you about an SMBH — its spacetime (spin), its meal plan (fallback light curves), or both — and which formation channel delivered that star already spinning?

No single paper owns Kerr spin *and* multi-flare dimming. Stack them and three threads appear: (1) **test-particle orbits** deep in the potential, (2) **repeating partial disruptions** as a stress test of stellar structure + spin, (3) **Hills binary capture** as the shared origin story that supplies tight, eccentric, pre-spun stars. The tensions live where a spin *forecast* is mistaken for a spin *measurement*, or where Hills is treated as uniquely proven rather than the most natural channel consistent with dimming.

This synthesis is **SMBH–star dynamics**. AGN fuel, winds, duals, and cavities live on [[black-hole-feedback-and-changing-look-agn]]. Waveform / siren / pulsar strong-field tests live on [[gravitational-wave-strong-field-probes]]. Semiclassical evaporation endpoints live on [[black-hole-evaporation-energy-conditions]].

## Thread A — Orbits as spin meters (Galactic Center)

**Paper:** [[s301-sgra-spin-sensitive-star]]

GRAVITY+ finds S301: ~8.7 yr period, $e\sim0.98$, pericenter ~140–150 Schwarzschild radii, $v\sim0.08c$ — about 10× closer than S2. That jump compounds into $(v/c)^3$ frame-dragging sensitivity. Mock data through ~2035 (plus future RV) forecast $\chi$ to ~±0.1 in favorable orientations.

**Honest limits:** no spin measured yet; two-fold sky-orientation ambiguity without RV; single-facility astrometry; forecast orientation-dependent.

**Bridge to Thread B:** Hills-like capture is a leading story for how you get such eccentric, tight orbits in the first place — the same channel Thread B needs for pre-spin.

## Thread B — Repeating partial TDEs and the pre-spin fix

**Paper:** [[stellar-spin-repeating-partial-tde]]

Hydro models starting from zero spin struggled to produce many successively *dimmer* flares: tidal torque spun stars up until later encounters got brighter or destroyed the star. Initialize $\lambda\sim0.7$–0.8 near $\Omega_p$ (Hills-natural) → spin plateaus → densification of the stripped massive core drives monotonic dimming over ≥4 passages.

**Honest limits:** single-group SPH lineage; retrograde unexplored; AT2018fyk-class ~10× drops need extra physics.

## Thread C — Shared Hills hinge

Both threads lean on **Hills binary tidal disruption**: one star ejected as a hypervelocity runaway, the other captured on a tight eccentric SMBH orbit **already spinning** from tidal locking. Thread A needs the orbit; Thread B needs the spin. Neither page uniquely proves Hills — both are “most natural channel” arguments.
