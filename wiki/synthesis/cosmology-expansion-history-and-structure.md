# Cosmology: Expansion History and Large-Scale Structure

**One-line summary:** This wiki’s late-universe cosmology pages form a comparative map of **expansion-history probes** (BAO, SN Ia, sirens, time-delay cosmography) against **structure and principle tests** (cosmic web, ultra-large anomalies, force-law checks) and a few **early-universe levers** ($τ$, LQC shear) — without crowning a dark-energy microphysics or dumping PeVatron messengers into the same catalog.

## Why pull these together

DESI, SN Ia systematics, lensed SLSNe, GW sirens, JWST density maps, and Mg II mega-structures look like separate subfields. In this wiki they answer one systems question:

> Which observable measures **how fast the universe expands**, which measures **how matter is arranged**, and which early knob ($τ$, bounce shear) can fake or fix a late-time tension?

No single paper owns $H_0$, $w(z)$, and the Cosmological Principle. Stack them and three axes appear: (1) **tape measures of expansion**, (2) **maps and anomalies of structure**, (3) **early levers and alternatives** that compete with a pure DE fluid. The tensions live where a “3–4σ preference” depends on which SN sample you feed the fit, or where a 400 Mpc ring is either a principle crisis or a look-elsewhere artifact.

This synthesis is **data-driven expansion history and structure**. Speculative / modified gravity approaches (dRGT, warp, ontology rewrites, MOND as theory) live on [[modified-speculative-gravity]] — that page may cite DESI as a *constraint*, but this page owns the phenomenology. High-energy messengers, PeVatrons, and ISM chemistry live on [[high-energy-astrophysics-multimessenger]]. Dark-matter *search channels* live on [[dark-matter-detection-channels]].

## Thread A — Expansion history: rulers, candles, sirens, delays

**Papers:** [[desi-evolving-dark-energy]], [[cigars-i-supernova-cosmology]], [[holismokes-sn-winny]], [[gw170817-jet-hubble]], [[universe-gas-pedal-leaky]]  
**Concepts:** [[baryon-acoustic-oscillations]], [[dark-energy-equation-of-state]], [[hubble-tension]], [[time-delay-cosmography]]

### A1 — DESI BAO prefers evolving dark energy

[[desi-evolving-dark-energy]]: multi-tracer BAO across ~14M galaxies/quasars (DR2 science core) prefers $w_0 w_a$CDM over $\Lambda$CDM at roughly 3–4σ **when combined with CMB and leading SN Ia sets**, with $w_a < 0$ (DE weaker today than in the past). Significance is **SN-sample dependent**.

**Intuition:** The BAO cookie-cutter (~150 Mpc) is a tape measure. A pure cosmological constant is a fixed throttle. DESI’s tape prefers a throttle that eased off.

**Limits:** $w_0$–$w_a$ is an ansatz, not a unique microphysics; $r_d$ anchors to early-universe physics; full 5-year catalog still forthcoming.

### A2 — SN Ia systematics are not a cosmetic patch

[[cigars-i-supernova-cosmology]]: hierarchical forward model (galaxies → progenitors → light/dust/selection) + set-based neural ratio estimation recovers cosmology on mocks and attributes the classic host **mass step** largely to metallicity/age physics.

**Why it matters here:** DESI’s evolving-$w$ preference *depends on which SN dataset you attach*. CIGaRS-style host physics is therefore not optional cleanup — it feeds the DE equation-of-state debate.

### A3 — Independent geometric paths (still thin)

- [[holismokes-sn-winny]]: first galaxy-scale strongly lensed SLSN (Winny, $z\approx2$, five images) — foundation for future time-delay $H_0$; **delays not yet measured**; models not cosmography-grade.
- [[gw170817-jet-hubble]]: still the only GW event with a named host for siren $H_0$; jet geometry tightens inclination systematics.

**A-thread tension:** BAO+SN prefers evolving DE; sirens and time delays target $H_0$ with different systematics. They are **complementary**, not automatic mutual confirmation. [[universe-gas-pedal-leaky]] sits as a neighboring acceleration narrative (thermodynamic / emergent-gravity flavor) — cite as constraint context, not as DESI’s microphysics.

**Handoff:** For massive gravitons, warp metrics, or ontology rewrites that *compete* with a DE fluid, open [[modified-speculative-gravity]].

## Thread B — Structure maps and Cosmological Principle stress tests

**Papers:** [[cosmos-web-cosmic-web]], [[big-ring-ultra-large-structure]], [[gigaparsec-anisotropic-structures]], [[newton-ksz-force-law]]  
**Concepts:** [[cosmic-web]], [[cosmological-principle]], [[ksz-effect]]

### B1 — JWST density ↔ star formation

[[cosmos-web-cosmic-web]]: contiguous JWST mapping of overdensity to $z\sim7$ with a reported SFR–density flip in dense environments — structure as an *environment machine*, not a BAO ruler.

### B2 — Ultra-large anomalies

[[big-ring-ultra-large-structure]] (~400 Mpc Mg II ring near the Giant Arc) and [[gigaparsec-anisotropic-structures]] ask whether rare structures or preferred directions stress the homogeneity/isotropy assumption. Post-hoc significance and tracer bias dominate the honest limits.

### B3 — Force law on megaparsec scales

[[newton-ksz-force-law]]: ACT+BOSS pairwise kSZ velocities recover a force-law exponent $n\approx2.1$ class — a large-scale “is gravity still $1/r^2$?” check that neighbors MOND/Alena discussions without settling them.

**B vs A:** Thread A changes the **throttle**. Thread B asks whether the **road network** and the **force that builds it** look standard. A 400 Mpc ring does not by itself prove evolving $w$; evolving $w$ does not by itself invent a Big Ring.
