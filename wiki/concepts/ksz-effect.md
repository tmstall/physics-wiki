# Kinematic Sunyaev–Zel’dovich (kSZ) Effect

**One-line summary:** Doppler shift of CMB photons scattering off moving hot gas — a radar gun for cluster bulk velocities in [[newton-ksz-force-law]], and for organized internal motions in [[rotational-ksz-cluster-motions]].
