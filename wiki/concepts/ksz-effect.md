# Kinematic Sunyaev–Zel’dovich (kSZ) Effect

**One-line summary:** Doppler shift of CMB photons scattering off moving hot gas — a radar gun for cluster bulk velocities, used in [[newton-ksz-force-law]] to read the force law.
