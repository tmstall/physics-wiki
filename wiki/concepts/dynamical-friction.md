# Dynamical Friction

**One-line summary:** Gravitational drag a massive body feels as it plows through a sea of lighter stars or gas, waking a density wake that pulls backward and drains orbital energy.

## Physical picture

A SMBH moving through a galactic disk raises a trailing overdensity; that overdensity tugs the hole backward. Coplanar orbits feel strong, frequent drag; highly inclined orbits sample the dense midplane less often—and can even experience torques that *increase* tilt ([[smbh-inclination-angle]]).
