# Time-Delay Cosmography

**One-line summary:** Measure differential light-travel times among lensed images plus a lens mass model to extract \(H_0\) from pure geometry — independent of the distance ladder and the CMB.

## Physical picture

Different paths through the lens take different times. Delays scale with angular-diameter distance ratios \(\propto 1/H_0\). Proposed by Refsdal (1964); [[holismokes-sn-winny]] is a promising SLSN realization still awaiting measured delays and cosmography-grade models.

A second geometric handle does not need a delay: two sources at different distances behind one lens ([[muse-desi-lens-confirmation]] double-plane systems). The *ratio* of the two Einstein rings depends only on distance ratios, so it probes expansion history without the lens-mass absolute scale. Imaging and mass models still dominate the error budget.
