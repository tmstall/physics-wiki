# Black Hole Interiors

**One-line summary:** The causal region behind horizons — classically riddled with singularities and Cauchy horizons; semiclassical and topological ideas try to keep it describable.

## Physical picture

Four wiki threads:

- **Causal structure:** [[evaporating-charged-black-holes]] asks whether evaporation + repulsion can remove singularities and Cauchy horizons.
- **Topological constraints:** [[frozen-in-gravitational-fields]] asks what curvature connectivity can do as the geometry evolves.
- **Double-copy thermality:** [[hawking-radiation-charge-shell]] recovers a Hawking-like spectrum from a flat-space charge shell.
- **Early-universe origin:** [[pre-bang-leftovers]] / [[primordial-black-holes]] ask how horizons might form before or at a cosmic bounce (relic DM candidates).
- **Horizonless mimickers:** [[gravastar-dust-collapse]] / [[gravastars]] show a pure-GR dynamical path to a de Sitter-core object (fine-tuned).
- **Thermodynamic / observational horizon variables:** [[entropy-maximization-bh-mergers]], [[horizon-direct-wave-gw250114]], [[black-hole-thermodynamics]].

Horizons as special multiparty geometry also appear in [[quantum-jamming]].
