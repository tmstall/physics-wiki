# Type Ia Supernovae

**One-line summary:** Thermonuclear white-dwarf explosions used as standardizable candles to map cosmic expansion — residual host-dependent brightness is the systematic that modern analyses must model, not patch.

## Physical picture

After light-curve shape and color corrections, peak brightness is nearly standard, so apparent magnitude yields distance. Host-galaxy properties (mass, metallicity, age, dust) still shift residuals. The classic Mass Step is an empirical offset; [[cigars-i-supernova-cosmology]] replaces it with progenitor metallicity/age physics inside a forward model.

SN Ia samples also anchor late-universe expansion when combined with BAO: DESI’s preference for evolving dark energy over pure \(\Lambda\) is SN-dataset dependent ([[desi-evolving-dark-energy]], [[dark-energy-equation-of-state]], [[baryon-acoustic-oscillations]]). Better host/progenitor modeling is therefore not only a ladder issue — it feeds the dark-energy equation-of-state debate.
