# Collective Tunneling

**One-line summary:** Strongly interacting atoms can hop through a barrier only as a whole composite when partial transfer is energetically off-resonance — enabling massive center-of-mass superpositions without the naive exponential mass death of single-particle tunneling.

## Physical picture

Textbook tunneling dies exponentially with \(\sqrt{m}\) for a fixed barrier. That is the free-particle story. In a Mott-filled double well, on-site repulsion \(U\) makes “leave one atom behind” costly. The resonant channel is often:

**all \(n\) left** ↔ **all \(n\) right**.

When single-particle hopping \(J_0\) is comparable to \(U\), the cluster is rigid enough to move as one object on the tunneling timescale, and the rate can stay nearly flat as \(n\) grows.

Everyday analogy: a revolving door that only accepts a complete group — individuals bounce; the legal move is everyone switching booths together.

## Why it matters

[[massive-tunneling-schrodinger-cats]] uses this to put ~608 u Rb clusters into spatial cat / NOON states and run quantum-enhanced Ramsey sensing. It is a scalable *strategy*, not only a mass record — if the exponential base approaches 1, adding atoms does not kill the experiment.
