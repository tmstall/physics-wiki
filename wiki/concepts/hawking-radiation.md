# Hawking Radiation

**One-line summary:** Semiclassical black holes emit thermal radiation; the paired negative-energy flux into the horizon violates classical energy conditions and can reshape causal structure during evaporation.

## Physical picture

Outgoing positive-energy particles and ingoing negative-energy flux are two sides of the same quantum process near the horizon. In [[evaporating-charged-black-holes]], that null-energy violation is the ingredient that can turn a would-be eternal trapped region into a temporary one — enabling regular evaporation endpoints when combined with charge or spin repulsion. A complementary flat-space route to the same thermality uses the [[double-copy]] of a collapsing shell ([[hawking-radiation-charge-shell]]).
