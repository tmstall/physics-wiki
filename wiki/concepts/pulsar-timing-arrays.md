# Pulsar Timing Arrays (PTAs)

**One-line summary:** Networks of millisecond pulsars used as Galactic-scale clocks to detect nanohertz gravitational waves from supermassive black-hole binaries—and, secondarily, to weigh satellites via acceleration systematics.

## Physical picture

A passing nanohertz GW stretches the Earth–pulsar path and imprints a correlated timing residual pattern across the array. The stochastic background is thought to be dominated by cosmic SMBH binaries; individual nearby inspirals (e.g. claimed Mrk 501-class systems) may eventually stand out as continuous waves. Single-binary laboratories like [[psr-j1906-binary-timing]] (young relativistic pair) and acceleration-based mass work ([[pulsars-satellite-masses]]) share the same TOA craft even when they are not array members.
