# Photometric Redshifts

**One-line summary:** Infer a galaxy’s redshift from broadband colors instead of a spectrum — cheap and scalable, but tangled with dust, age, mass, and template degeneracies.

## Physical picture

Guessing speed from one blurry photo of car color and shape: possible only if you know the fleet and road conditions well. Large JWST/LSST samples live or die on photo-\(z\) quality. [[cosmos-web-cosmic-web]] weights full \(P(z)\) into density maps; [[cigars-i-supernova-cosmology]] jointly infers photo-\(z\) inside a physics-based hierarchical model (~0.01 median scatter on mocks). High-\(z\) lensed transients such as [[holismokes-sn-winny]] still need spectroscopic redshifts for precision cosmography, but sit in the same photo-survey era.
