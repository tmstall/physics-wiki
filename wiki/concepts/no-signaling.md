# No-Signaling (and Superluminal Causation)

**One-line summary:** No-signaling forbids using nonlocal correlations to send a controllable message; multiparty scenarios can separate that rule from a stronger ban on any superluminal causal influence.

## Physical picture

- **NSS (no superluminal signaling):** you cannot *use* the correlations as a telegraph.
- **NSC (no superluminal causation):** there is no hidden faster-than-light causal wiring at all.

In two-party Bell tests the two often coincide. With three or more parties and nontrivial spacetime order, they can come apart — the gap where [[quantum-jamming]] lives.

Bell-certified Randomness Amplification assumes adversaries respect no-signaling (cannot pre-arrange outcomes using FTL influence).
