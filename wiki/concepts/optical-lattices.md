# Optical Lattices and Superlattices

**One-line summary:** Standing laser waves create periodic potential wells for ultracold atoms — an egg-carton of traps; a superlattice stacks two periods to form arrays of tunable double wells.

## Physical picture

Lattice spacing is set by laser wavelength (hundreds of nm). Depth is set by intensity. A **superlattice** adds a second period so each unit cell is a double well: internal barrier height and left–right tilt are knobs. Hundreds of identical cells run in parallel — built-in statistics.

## Role here

[[massive-tunneling-schrodinger-cats]] loads Mott-insulator fillings into superlattice double wells, then opens the internal barrier to watch collective tunneling and build cat states.
