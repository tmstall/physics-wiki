# Color Centers (Diamond Group-IV)

**One-line summary:** Atomic-scale defects in diamond (NV, SiV, GeV, SnV, …) that host optical transitions and spin qubits for quantum networking and sensing.

## Physical picture

A foreign atom plus vacancies create localized electronic levels. Group-IV centers (inversion-symmetric) resist first-order electric-field noise and offer stable optical lines. The **tin-vacancy (SnV⁻)** is the workhorse of [[snv-super-coherent-excitation]]: good spin coherence at cryogenic temperature, sharp optics, and a path to spin–photon interfaces. The **silicon-vacancy (SiV⁻)** is mapped under hydrostatic strain in [[siv-hydrostatic-strain-symmetry]] — continuous tuning until a tensile symmetry flip.
