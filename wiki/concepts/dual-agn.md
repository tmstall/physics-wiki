# Dual AGN / Dual Quasars

**One-line summary:** Two actively accreting supermassive black holes in one merging system—observational waypoints on the path from galaxy merger to hard SMBH binary and gravitational waves.

## Physical picture

Each nucleus lights up when its hole is fed. Confirming two *real* hosts (not lensed images) needs resolved host gas/stars or dual-engine kinematics (tidal bridges, double jets). Duals at high \(z\) constrain early merger rates; nearby duals test final-parsec physics ([[high-z-quasar-pair-merger]], [[mrk501-double-jet-smbbh]]).

**Compact / early-universe analog habitat:** [[dual-agn-green-pea]] extends confirmed duals into a Green Pea host (small, metal-poor, intense SF) — proof of existence outside the usual massive gas-rich merger sample, with Chandra+Keck multi-factor confirmation rather than ambiguous double-peaked lines alone.
