# Stochastic Gravitational-Wave Background (SGWB)

**One-line summary:** An incoherent superposition of gravitational waves from many early-Universe sources — a random noise bath on spacetime geometry, targeted by LIGO in the audio band, by PTAs at nanohertz, and by LISA in between.

## Physical picture

Not a single binary chirp: phase-transition bubbles, cosmic strings, primordial turbulence, etc. Anisotropic, time-varying metric perturbations. In [[gw-induced-fermion-freeze-in]] that texture sources one-loop fermion production.

[[lvk-o4a-cosmological-gwb]] is the audio-band measurement: one O1–O4a null, energy density below about $3 \times 10^{-9}$ for a flat spectrum, then re-read as eight templates. Silence here does not close the nanohertz PTA window or the models whose peak sits outside 20–100 Hz.
