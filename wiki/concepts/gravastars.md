# Gravastars

**One-line summary:** Horizonless black-hole mimickers with a de Sitter (dark-energy) interior and a thin matter shell—looking almost like a black hole outside while avoiding a classical singularity and event horizon.

## Physical picture

Instead of collapsing to a point, the core becomes a tiny accelerating “baby universe” held by a thin shell. From far away, light bending and orbits can mimic a black hole. [[gravastar-dust-collapse]] shows one pure-GR dynamical birth channel from fine-tuned dust collapse (compactness below \(3/8\)).
