# Hubble Tension

**One-line summary:** Local distance-ladder \(H_0\) (~73 km/s/Mpc) disagrees with early-universe ΛCDM/CMB inferences (~67 km/s/Mpc) at high significance — a third, independent method is urgently needed.

## Physical picture

[[time-delay-cosmography]] with lensed supernovae is one proposed third path. [[holismokes-sn-winny]] aims at that program; [[cigars-i-supernova-cosmology]] attacks SN systematics on the ladder side. Separately, [[newton-ksz-force-law]] stress-tests the large-scale gravitational *force law* (not \(H_0\) itself) with kSZ pairwise velocities — a related “is GR still right on megaparsec scales?” check.

DESI’s BAO expansion history ([[desi-evolving-dark-energy]], [[baryon-acoustic-oscillations]], [[dark-energy-equation-of-state]]) now prefers *evolving* dark energy over pure \(\Lambda\). That does not automatically fix \(H_0\), but it weakens the assumption that early- and late-universe parameters must lock under fixed-\(\Lambda\) CDM.

Another early-universe lever is the CMB electron-scattering optical depth $\tau$. A Pop III.1 “Flash” ionization at $z\sim20$–$25$ ([[early-universe-popiii-flash-ionization]]) can raise $\tau$ by $\sim0.04$ and, in some analyses, relax the CMB preference toward lower $H_0$ — at the cost of tension with Planck’s low-$\tau$ window and reliance on unproven supermassive first stars. Treat as a speculative reionization fix, not a settled $H_0$ solution.
