# Cosmic Web

**One-line summary:** Gravity organizes matter into clusters, filaments, walls, and voids — a traffic network that actively steers galaxy growth and quenching across cosmic time.

## Physical picture

Overdensity $\delta$ measures how busy a patch is relative to the cosmic mean. Early dense nodes act as gas pumps; later they become quenching zones. [[cosmos-web-cosmic-web]] maps this with JWST COSMOS-Web to $z \sim 7$ and pins an SFR–density flip reversal near $z \approx 1.8$. Deep Ly$\alpha$ imaging can light the gaseous filament itself between quasars ([[muse-quasar-filament-z3]]). On much smaller scales, stellar nurseries live in Giant Molecular Clouds whose ice chemistry is mapped in [[interstellar-glaciers-spherex]] — the cold “fuel pipes” that eventually feed the web’s galaxies. Local AGN feeding filaments ([[jwst-filament-cnd-ngc4696]]) are the last-kiloparsec version of the same supply-line idea.

Before the web’s galaxy factories dominate reionization, a proposed Pop III.1 “Flash” ([[early-universe-popiii-flash-ionization]]) can ionize ~cMpc bubbles at $z\sim20$–$25$ and plant heavy SMBH seeds at web nodes — an early electron fog that CMB photons still scatter on today.
