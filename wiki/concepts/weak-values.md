# Weak Values

**One-line summary:** Conditional averages of an observable between pre- and post-selected states — allowed to lie outside the eigenvalue spectrum, including negative “times” that still match experiment.

## Physical picture

Not ordinary expectation values. Post-select on a rare outcome (e.g. photon transmitted), weakly measure an interaction along the way, and the conditional average can look paradoxical while remaining fully quantum and causal overall. [[negative-weak-valued-excitation-times]] measures negative atomic excitation dwell times equal to negative [[group-delay]].
