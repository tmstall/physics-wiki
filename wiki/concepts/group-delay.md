# Group Delay

**One-line summary:** \(\tau_g = d\phi/d\omega\) — how much a pulse peak lags (or leads) after traversing a medium; near resonance it can be negative via anomalous dispersion without superluminal signaling.

## Physical picture

Pulse reshaping and interference, not information time travel. [[negative-weak-valued-excitation-times]] shows the weak-valued atomic excitation time for transmitted photons equals \(\tau_g\), including the negative branch.
