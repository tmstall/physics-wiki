# Alena Tensor

**One-line summary:** A dual stress-energy formalism that rewrites curved-path motion as geodesic motion (and vice versa); when rotational energy is included, galaxy outskirts gain an effective \(1/r^2\) mass density that can mimic a dark halo.

## Physical picture

Standard GR dust forgets collective spin. The Alena Tensor builds a complete continuum source—including vorticity—so rest mass plus ~2× rotational energy gravitates. Outer disks then look isothermal-halo-like without new particles. The same algebraic object has a flat-space / quantum-vortex reading (structural so far).

## Key results and tensions

- SPARC rotation curves: competitive one-parameter fits for a large fraction of the sample ([[alena-tensor-rotation-dm]]).
- Does **not** yet replace particle DM or MOND on clusters, CMB, or BAO.
- Large-scale force-law tests prefer inverse-square gravity ([[newton-ksz-force-law]]), which constrains modified-force alternatives more than incomplete \(T^{\mu\nu}\) bookkeeping—but lensing and cluster tests still required for Alena.
