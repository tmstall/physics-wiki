# Second-Order Doppler Shift (Time Dilation in Clocks)

**One-line summary:** A moving clock ticks slower by a factor of roughly \(v^2/2c^2\); in ion clocks this kinetic time dilation is a tiny fractional frequency shift proportional to kinetic energy over rest energy.

## Physical picture

Special relativity: proper time advances more slowly for a clock with speed \(v\). For non-relativistic ions the fractional frequency shift is proportional to kinetic energy divided by \(mc^2\). In a thermal cloud you average that shift and call it a **systematic error**. In a quantum trap, kinetic energy is an **operator** with discrete eigenvalues (phonon ladder), so the shift becomes state-dependent and can entangle motion with the internal clock.

GPS analogy: satellites need relativistic corrections. Quantum version: the satellite is smeared across several velocity states at once, so the correction is not a single number.

## Role in the wiki

- Classical SODS: metrology nuisance to calibrate.
- Quantum SODS / proper time: central signal in [[time-goes-quantum]] and [[quantum-proper-time-ion-clocks]] (including vacuum and squeezing-induced variants: vSODS, sqSODS, qSODS).
