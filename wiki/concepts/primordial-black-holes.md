# Primordial Black Holes (PBHs)

**One-line summary:** Black holes formed in the early universe rather than from stellar collapse — standard inflation rare-overdensity channel vs bounce-survival channels in [[pre-bang-leftovers]]; the Dark Dimension variant ([[primordial-black-holes-are-5d]]) turns every realistic channel into a 5D hole below ~$10^{23}$ g.

## Physical picture

PBHs are an *origin* story (when and how a horizon forms in the early cosmos). Late-time questions about whether evaporating holes stay regular live on [[black-hole-interiors]] and [[black-hole-evaporation-energy-conditions]] — different chapter, same objects if PBHs survive long enough.

A 4D hole’s Hawking bookkeeping sets the evaporating-DM window used by [[gamma-glow-pbh-detector]]. [[primordial-black-holes-are-5d]] says that if space hides a micron extra dimension, no standard mechanism makes a hole heavier than the Gregory–Laflamme crossover, so the holes that *could* form are 5D, colder, and longer-lived. That is a mass ceiling inside one speculative scenario, not a detection, and it weakens 4D γ-glow limits only if the extra dimension is real.
