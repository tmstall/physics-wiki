# Monogamy of Entanglement

**One-line summary:** Maximal entanglement is a finite budget — if Alice is fully entangled with Bob, she cannot also be fully entangled with Charlie.

## Physical picture

Think of a marriage license for quantum correlations. Spread thinner across partners and each pairwise link weakens. Device-independent QKD uses monogamy as a tripwire: an eavesdropper who tries to share entanglement necessarily degrades Alice–Bob Bell violations.

[[quantum-jamming]] asks whether some post-quantum, still “no-signaling” theories can rewrite correlations in ways that evade monogamy while looking local to each party. Constructive device-independent work that *relies* on Bell monogamy structure includes [[certified-randomness-amplification]].
