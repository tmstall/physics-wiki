# Ultra-Metal-Poor Stars

**One-line summary:** Ancient low-mass stars with heavy-element fractions \(\ll 10^{-3}\)–\(10^{-4}\) solar that chemically encode individual early supernovae and the accretion history of the Milky Way and its satellites.

## Physical picture

The first stars (Population III) were massive and short-lived; their explosions seeded later generations. Surviving UMP / VMP stars are low-mass fossils of those seeds. Abundance patterns (C, Fe-peak, r-/s-process) are barcodes of one or few progenitors; orbits tell which dwarf or stream delivered them.

## Key results

- [[ancient-immigrant-lmc-star]]: SDSS J0715−7334 — extreme UMP, LMC-associated, single Pop III SN-like pattern; dust-cooling implication.
- [[loki-early-accreted-vmp]]: planar VMP population as early-accreted MW fossil (“Loki”).
