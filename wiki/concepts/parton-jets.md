# Parton Jets

**One-line summary:** Collimated sprays of hadrons from high-momentum quarks or gluons — the smoking gun of hard QCD scattering, subtle to claim cleanly in purely hadronic final states of the ISR era.

## Physical picture

A hard-scattered parton fragments into a spray. Inclusive high-\(p_T\) triggers often sit near the tip of that spray (\(Z_\mathrm{trig}\) large). Modern colliders reconstruct jets with calibrated algorithms; early ISR work built the case from scaling, correlations, and \(P_\mathrm{out}\)-style observables under heavy systematics — outline in [[levinthal-high-pt-isr]], fuller narrative in [[high-pt-physics-cern-isr]].

**Modern hard-probe cousins in this wiki:** ultra-peripheral J/ψ photoproduction with spin interference ([[star-jpsi-spin-interference]]) asks a related question — what is pointlike and coherent inside nuclei — without reconstructing a hadronic jet cone. Keep ISR jets and UPC gluon tomography as the same **hard-probe ladder**, different final states ([[nuclear-dense-matter-precision]] Thread C).

**Proton structure bookkeeping:** [[intrinsic-charm-proton-nnpdf]] isolates a non-radiative charm PDF component that feeds forward $Z+$charm-jet and other large-$x$ collider predictions — same parton ladder, structure-function side rather than jet reconstruction.

**Where the lost momentum goes:** [[cms-jet-diffusion-wake]] is not a jet-reconstruction paper. It uses a large η gap between dijets to expose a ~1% soft-particle deficit in central PbPb, the diffusion wake the medium should leave opposite a quenched jet. Quenching measures what the parton loses. The wake asks where that momentum reappears. Five standard deviations establish the dip. Naming it the wake is still an inference, and the models match the trend better than the depth.
