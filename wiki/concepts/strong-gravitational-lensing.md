# Strong Gravitational Lensing

**One-line summary:** A foreground mass bends light enough to produce multiple distinct images (or rings/arcs) of a background source — the geometry behind time-delay cosmography.

## Physical picture

Wine glass in front of a candle: multiple distorted copies. Binary lens galaxies can create five images ([[holismokes-sn-winny]]). Image positions constrain the mass model; delays constrain \(H_0\).

An image that *looks* like a lens is not a lens until redshifts say the arc sits far behind the galaxy. [[muse-desi-lens-confirmation]] is the spectroscopic factory for that test: 55 southern DESI neural-net candidates get a lens+source pair, 6 fail as spiral arms, tidal tails, or foreground blobs. Double-source-plane systems in that catalog are the distance-ratio path that does not need the lens mass normalization.

**Neighbor (not cosmography):** [[pks2233-neutrino-lensing]] explores whether jet jumps / light-curve symmetry in an IceCube blazar candidate need microlensing or self-lensing — macrolensing (split images) is ruled out there; remaining lensing stories stay illustrative. [[fdm-wave-lensing-hs0810]] is wave-halo structure on a known lens, not a confirmation catalog.
