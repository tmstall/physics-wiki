# Coherent Forward Scattering

**One-line summary:** Collective, phase-coherent scattering of light into the forward direction by an atomic ensemble — the process that shapes transmitted pulses and underpins optical depth effects near resonance.

## Physical picture

A “transmitted” photon is not a non-interacting tourist; it is the amplitude that constructively interferes after interacting with the cloud. That interference is why weak-valued dwell times can track [[group-delay]], including negative values in [[negative-weak-valued-excitation-times]].
