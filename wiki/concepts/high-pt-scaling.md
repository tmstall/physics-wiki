# High-\(p_T\) Scaling

**One-line summary:** Inclusive single-particle spectra at large transverse momentum follow power-law scaling forms whose effective exponent \(n\) diagnoses hard partonic scattering versus softer multi-parton pictures.

## Physical picture

Soft hadronic physics predicts exponential falloff. A power-law tail means something pointlike is scattering inside the proton. Scaling variables such as \(X_t\) organize data across beam energies; changes in local \(n\) mark transitions between dynamical regimes. Historical ISR analyses ([[levinthal-high-pt-isr]], fuller narrative in [[high-pt-physics-cern-isr]]) measured this transition during the birth of the QCD jet paradigm.
