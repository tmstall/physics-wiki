# Magnons

**One-line summary:** Quantized spin waves—collective precession quanta that carry angular momentum without net charge transport; building blocks of magnonic signal processing and nonlinear mode competition.

## Physical picture

Flip one spin in an ordered magnet and the disturbance propagates. Quantize it: a magnon. At large amplitude, four-magnon scattering and Kerr/cross-Kerr shifts make modes talk to each other like optical nonlinearities in a fiber.

**Textures vs waves:** Chiral magnets host larger topological spin textures (skyrmions, heliknotons) whose dynamics still ride on the same exchange and Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya physics that set magnon spectra ([[magnetic-heliknoton-electric-write]], [[spin-flip-flop-saf]]). A heliknoton is a **static (or slowly driven) soliton** with a Hopf index; magnons are the **small oscillations** around the ordered state. Writing a knot with spin-transfer torque and hopping energy between magnon modes in a synthetic antiferromagnet are different control knobs on the same material family.

**What magnons are not:** They are not fractional charges, not photonic non-Abelian braids, and not quantum-metric nonlinearities in oxide 2DEGs — those live in other threads of [[condensed-matter-topology-fractionalization]].
