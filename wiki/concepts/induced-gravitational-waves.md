# Induced Gravitational Waves

**One-line summary:** Second-order tensor modes sourced by first-order (usually scalar) cosmological perturbations—candidate stochastic backgrounds from the early universe, sensitive to gauge choices until mapped to observables.

## Physical picture

First-order density waves can “square” into a random gravitational-wave fog. At second order the math is coordinate-sensitive; [[second-order-gw-strain-gauge]] argues the physical strain free-falling observers measure matches Newton-gauge TT waves. Connects to [[stochastic-gw-background]] and freeze-in channels ([[gw-induced-fermion-freeze-in]]).
