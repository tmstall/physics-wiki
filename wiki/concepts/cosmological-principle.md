# Cosmological Principle

**One-line summary:** The working assumption that the universe is homogeneous and isotropic on sufficiently large scales—the foundation of FLRW cosmology—and under observational stress if ultra-large structures are real.

## Physical picture

Zoom out enough and every patch should look like every other. Surveys map the cosmic web; claims of ~Gpc arcs and rings ([[big-ring-ultra-large-structure]]) test where “enough” really is versus ΛCDM variance and selection effects.
