# Supermassive Black Hole Binaries

**One-line summary:** Paired SMBHs after galaxy mergers that must lose orbital energy via dynamical friction, stellar scattering, gas disks, and eventually gravitational waves—often stalling if the kiloparsec-scale channel fails.

## Physical picture

Two galaxies merge; two central black holes sink. Dynamical friction dominates at kpc scales; the last-parsec problem and GW-driven plunge take over later. Orbital inclination relative to a remnant disk is a hard filter on whether pairing completes within a Hubble time ([[smbh-inclination-angle]]).

Observational anchors: high-\(z\) dual quasars ([[high-z-quasar-pair-merger]]), Euclid’s early quasar census ([[euclid-high-z-quasar-census]]), a close blazar double-jet candidate ([[mrk501-double-jet-smbbh]]), and an ultramassive core-scouring cavity ([[ultramassive-bh-binary-cavity]]). PTA nanohertz backgrounds may be the integrated GW drone of such systems ([[pulsar-timing-arrays]]).

**Seed channel:** Pop III.1 supermassive stars can plant $\sim10^5\,M_\odot$ heavy seeds at $z\sim20$–$25$ while flash-ionizing the IGM ([[early-universe-popiii-flash-ionization]]) — a formation path that sets the comoving SMBH density before merger-driven pairing begins.
