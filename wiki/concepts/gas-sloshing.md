# Gas Sloshing

**One-line summary:** Bulk oscillatory motion of the cool dense core of a group/cluster halo after a gravitational disturbance (minor merger), producing spiral cold fronts and dragging metal-rich gas outward.

## Physical picture

Bump a coffee cup: dense liquid overshoots and wraps. In X-rays, contact discontinuities (cold fronts) mark the wrapping layers—often metal-enhanced inside ([[ic1262-metal-mixing]]).
