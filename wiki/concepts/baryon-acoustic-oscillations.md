# Baryon Acoustic Oscillations (BAO)

**One-line summary:** A frozen sound-horizon scale (~150 Mpc today) imprinted in the galaxy correlation function acts as a standard ruler for the expansion history at many redshifts.

## Physical picture

Before recombination, photon–baryon plasma supported sound waves. At decoupling the waves froze; galaxies later prefer to sit ~one sound-horizon apart. Measuring that preferred separation transversely and radially gives \(D_M/r_d\) and \(D_H/r_d\)—a tape measure of cosmic expansion.

## Key results

- DESI multi-tracer BAO (BGS, LRG, ELG, QSO, Lyα) is the current precision engine ([[desi-evolving-dark-energy]]).
- Combined with CMB + SN Ia, BAO drives the evolving-dark-energy tension with pure \(\Lambda\).
