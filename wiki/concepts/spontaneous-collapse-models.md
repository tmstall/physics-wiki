# Spontaneous Collapse Models

**One-line summary:** Objective, continuous localization of the wavefunction (CSL, Diósi–Penrose, …) replaces observer-triggered collapse; mass-density coupling to stochastic noise can be read as gravitational-potential jitter and therefore as a floor on proper-time precision.

## Physical picture

Textbook QM never says *when* a superposition becomes a fact. Collapse models add a physical, mass-weighted “always-on” localization. Big objects decohere almost instantly; microscopic systems barely notice. Leading variants: Continuous Spontaneous Localization (random hits smeared over length \(r_C\)) and Diósi–Penrose (rate set by gravitational self-energy of superposed mass distributions).

## Key results

- Interpreting collapse noise as Newtonian \(\phi\) fluctuations yields \(\sqrt{t}\) time jitter many orders below optical clocks ([[collapse-models-clock-precision]]).
- Compatible with precision metrology while still linking the measurement problem to gravity and time.
