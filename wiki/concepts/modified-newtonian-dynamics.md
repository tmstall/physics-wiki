# Modified Newtonian Dynamics (MOND)

**One-line summary:** Phenomenology in which gravity’s force law softens at ultra-low accelerations (~10⁻¹⁰ m/s²), often approaching ~1/\(r\) on large scales — disfavored at 30–230 Mpc by [[newton-ksz-force-law]] (\(n\approx 2\) vs \(n\approx 1\)).

## Related alternatives

- Incomplete stress-energy bookkeeping (rotational energy as “halo”): [[alena-tensor]], [[alena-tensor-rotation-dm]] — different claim from MOND; still needs cluster/CMB tests.
- Large-scale inverse-square recovery: [[newton-ksz-force-law]], [[ksz-effect]]
- External Field Effect on SPARC (environment shapes outer curves): [[mond-external-field-sparc]] — strengthens MOND’s SEP-violating prediction; remains in tension with large-scale \(n\approx 2\) kSZ test.
