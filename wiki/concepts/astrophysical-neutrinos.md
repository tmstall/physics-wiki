# Astrophysical Neutrinos

**One-line summary:** High-energy neutrinos are undeflected, weakly interacting messengers of hadronic accelerators — IceCube maps a Galactic-plane glow and hunts individual blazar associations, still mostly extragalactic in the all-sky flux budget.

## Physical picture

When cosmic-ray nuclei hit gas, charged pions decay into neutrinos (and neutral pions into gamma rays). Neutrinos keep direction through magnetic fields and matter that scramble or absorb other messengers. The price is detection: cubic-kilometer targets (ice, seawater) and fierce atmospheric backgrounds.

Two wiki questions so far:

1. **Diffuse Galactic glow** — does the Milky Way light up in neutrinos like Fermi’s hadronic gamma plane? ([[icecube-galactic-plane-neutrinos]], ~4.5σ)
2. **Point-source candidates** — which blazars (if any) dominate local excesses? ([[pks2233-neutrino-lensing]], still post-trial weak)
