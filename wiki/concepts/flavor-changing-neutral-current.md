# Flavor-Changing Neutral Current (FCNC)

**One-line summary:** A process that changes quark (or lepton) flavor without changing electric charge—forbidden at tree level in the Standard Model and only allowed via rare loop “penguin” diagrams, making it a sharp probe of new heavy particles.

## Physical picture

A \(b\) quark cannot freely become an \(s\) quark while emitting a photon or \(\mu^+\mu^-\) at tree level. The allowed path loops through virtual W/top (and possibly unknown states). Because the SM rate is tiny, a modest new amplitude leaves a large relative fingerprint in branching ratios and angular observables.

## Key results

- LHCb \(B\to K^*\mu\mu\): persistent angular tension summarized by a downward shift in the vector Wilson coefficient \(C_9\) ([[b-meson-fcnc-anomaly]]).
