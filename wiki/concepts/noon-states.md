# NOON States (Spatial Schrödinger Cats)

**One-line summary:** A NOON state is a superposition of “all \(N\) particles on path A” and “all \(N\) on path B” — a maximally path-entangled cat useful for Heisenberg-limited phase sensing.

## Physical picture

Form: \((|N,0\rangle + e^{i\phi}|0,N\rangle)/\sqrt{2}\). There is no half-and-half classical mixture of particles; the entire cluster is left *and* right until you measure. Phase between the arms winds **\(N\) times faster** than for a single particle, so energy differences resolve more finely (Heisenberg scaling for fixed \(N\), with the usual fragility to loss).
