# Bell Tests

**One-line summary:** Spaced-apart parties measure entangled systems in random bases; correlations above classical bounds rule out local hidden-variable explanations of the outcomes.

## Physical picture

CHSH inequality: classical local realism caps a correlation score at 2; quantum mechanics allows up to \(2\sqrt{2}\). A **loophole-free** test closes the detection loophole (fair sampling) and the locality loophole (spacelike separation + fast measurement so no light-speed cheat).

Outcomes that violate Bell cannot have been fully pre-determined — they supply the certified randomness used in Randomness Amplification and in the experimental paper [[certified-randomness-amplification]]. Multiparty spacetime variants feed the [[quantum-jamming]] debate.
