# Motional Squeezing

**One-line summary:** Squeeze a trapped ion’s vibrational state so one quadrature (e.g. momentum or position) has reduced variance and the conjugate has increased variance — a controllable knob on relativistic frequency shifts and clock–motion entanglement.

## Physical picture

The trap ground state already has zero-point spread in position and momentum. A squeezed state trades one against the other. Because second-order Doppler physics depends on \(\langle P^2\rangle\) (momentum spread), squeezing **dials** the time-dilation shift (sqSODS) and can make proper-time entanglement more visible as Ramsey contrast loss.

## Why it matters here

[[quantum-proper-time-ion-clocks]] treats squeezing as the practical amplifier: no new ion species required — prepare motion better, read fringes carefully. Predicted ~7% visibility loss for realistic Al⁺ parameters sits near the edge of current systematics.
