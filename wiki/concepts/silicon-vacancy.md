# Silicon-Vacancy (SiV) Center

**One-line summary:** Group-IV diamond defect with inversion-symmetric D₃d geometry in equilibrium — clean optical lines and suppressed first-order electric-field noise; strain can tune it or flip it to NV-like C₃v.

## Physical picture

Silicon sits between two carbon vacancies, bonded to six carbons. Perfect balance means no permanent dipole until something (e.g. ~4% isotropic tension in [[siv-hydrostatic-strain-symmetry]]) breaks the symmetry.
